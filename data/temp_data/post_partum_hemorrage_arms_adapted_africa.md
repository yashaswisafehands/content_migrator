### Arms

A helper positioned by the arms should:

- Check pulse and blood pressure.

- Establish two large bore IV accesses.

- Cross-match and blood type if available.

- Start fluid resuscitation with 1-2 L or more of normal saline or Ringer’s lactate. Infuse rapidly, giving one bag at a time!

**Caution:** Excessive IV fluid administration can cause pulmonary oedema in women with preeclampsia.

<color style="blue">**Administer drugs:**</color>

Initial treatment with drugs:

- **Oxytocin** 10 IU IM/IV AND 
- **Tranexamic acid** 1 g slowly IV (give simultaneously as part of the initial care bundle).

If bleeding persists, administer additional drugs:

- **Oxytocin drip.**Mix oxytocin 20 IU in 1 L normal saline or Ringer's lactate and infuse as fast as possible. Then continue with a second bag, containing: oxytocin 20 IU in 1 L normal saline or Ringer’s lactate at 40 drops/minute. Do not give more than 3 L of IV fluid with oxytocin. 
- **Ergometrine**0.2 mg IV (if blood pressure is normal) 
- **Misoprostol**800 mcg sublingually or rectally if the bleeding continues and **oxytocin**is not available.

# Dosage and Administration

## Adults:

Cardiac arrest:

0.01 mg/kg intravenously followed by 20-50 ml normal saline push – can be repeated after 2 minutes.

Shock and anaphylactic reactions:

0.5 mg intramuscularly, can be repeated after 5-10 minutes OR

0.1 mg intravenously, can be repeated after 1-2 minutes

## Children:

The recommended intravenous dose in newborns is 0.1 to 0.3 mL/kg of a 1:10,000 solution (equal to 0.01 to 0.03 mg/kg). You will need to estimate the newborn’s weight after birth.

**How to prepare Epinephrine?**

Epinephrine is available as 1ml ampoule of 1:1000 concentration; however, for neonate take one ml of 1:1000 solution and add 9 ml of normal saline. This makes 10 ml of 1:10,000 concentration.

<color style="blue">Note: When the drug is given intravenously through a catheter, you should follow the drug with a 0.5 to 1ml flush of normal saline to be sure that the drug has reached the blood.</color>

# Contraindications

Narrow angle glaucoma, pheochromocytoma, low serum-potassium, tyreotoxicosis, and hypertension.

# Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are tachycardia, hypertension, tremor, headache, dizziness, anxiety, restlessness, and sleeplessness.

Uncommon adverse reactions are cardiac arrhythmia.

Rare adverse reactions are pulmonary edema and breathing difficulties.

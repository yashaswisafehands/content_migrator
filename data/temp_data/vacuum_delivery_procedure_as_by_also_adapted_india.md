### Procedure for vacuum extraction

##

Vacuum Extraction is a BEmONC Signal Function. However, the healthcare professionals are encouraged to follow the national guidance and should only perform procedures that you have been authorized for. Go through A-J

<color style="blue">**Ask** </color>for help.

<color style="blue">**Address** </color>the woman, tell her and birth companion what you are about to do while you continue to prepare, tell her that you need her to cooperate and keep pushing when there is a contraction.

<color style="blue">**Abdominal** </color>palpation – the head should not be palpable more than 1/5 above the pubic bone

<color style="blue">**Bladder** </color>empty.

<color style="blue">**Check** </color>that <color style**="blue">Cervix** </color>is fully dilated.

<color style="blue">**Contractions** </color>– there must be contractions to succeed.

<color style="blue">**Determine** </color>position – locate the posterior fontanel (triangular shaped) with your index finger. This is important to place the cup correctly.

<color style="blue">**Equipment** </color>ready – delivery tray, <color style**="blue">Oxytocin 10 IU** for IM injection</color> to the mother, towels, neonatal resuscitator, and vacuum extractor.

<color style="blue">**Flexion Point**</color> – most cups are 3 cm from the center to the edge. Place the edge of the cup at the tip of the posterior triangular fontanel.

<color style="blue">**Feel** </color>for vaginal tissue between the cup and the foetal skull before and after applying suction to avoid trauma to the vagina.

<color style="blue">**Gentle**</color>, steady traction with no rocking during a contraction; with the first contractions downward. During the following contractions, more upward.

<color style="blue">**Halt** </color>in between contractions.

<color style="blue">**HALT** </color>and abandon if three pop-offs, or if three pulls with no progress, or if 20 minutes from application delivery is not achieved.

<color style="blue">**Intact** </color>perineum!

When the head is delivered during the next contraction protect perineum with one hand; squeeze the index and thumb together at the posterior part of the head to prevent stretching of the perineum and lift the head with your third finger upwards and away from the perineum, while the other hand keeps the head from coming too fast.

An <color style="blue">Incision</color> is only rarely needed.

<color style="blue">**Jaw** </color>reachable – release the vacuum and remove the cup.

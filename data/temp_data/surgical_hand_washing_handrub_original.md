# Chapter: Practical information

### Algorithm for surgical hand washing (handrub)

(Standard operational procedure, approved be the Order of Ministry of Health #379 on 04.07.2014)

### Stage I – Surgical hand washing

1. Remove the rings and other decorations.
2. Wet and soap your hands, to cover the entire surface.
3. Remove dirt from under the nails using brushes or special nail cleaning sticks.
4. Keep wrists and carpus below your elbows during washing.
5. Follow the 6 steps of washing hands.
6. Wipe your hands, starting with your fingers, then wrist and carpus with a dry cloth.

### Stage II - Handrub

1. Apply handrub on the hands of 5-10 ml without touching the dispenser with the clean hands.
2. Use only the elbow dispenser with an antiseptic.
3. Apply handrub only on dry skin.
4. For surgical handrub, use alcohol-based, chlorehexidine based or other approved antiseptics that have a persistent effect.
5. Rub the antiseptic handrub until hands are completely dry. After treatment with antiseptic DO NOT USE TOWELS for drying.

See “procedure” on how to make alcohol-glycerine handrub mixture.

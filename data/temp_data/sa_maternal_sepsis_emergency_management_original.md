> CALL FOR HELP!

Call a **CAB**

### Circulation

Insert 2 wide bore IV cannulas.

Give at least 2L fluids IV,<color style="blue">**</color>normal sa**li**ne or Ringer’s lactate<**color style="blue"> </color>in the first hour.

Insert indwelling urine catheter to monitor in- and output.

### Airway

Position so airway is not occluded or at risk of aspiration.

### Breathing

Supplement with **oxygen**.

### Drugs

Antibiotics intravenous according to local guidelines, for example:

**Ampicillin 2 g/6 hrs** IV

AND

**Gentamicin 240 mg/24 hrs** IM

AND

**Metronidazole 500 mg/8 hrs** IV

If in a **malaria risk area**, treat according to local guidelines.

**Chloramphenicol**should be avoided in pregnant and lactating women.

# Chapter: Indication and usage

Doxycycline is a tetracycline-class antibacterial used to treat a wide variety of bacterial infections.

Administered as prophylactic antibiotic prior to Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) for non-lactating woman without sepsis.

# Dosage and Administration

## Antibiotic prophylaxis for abortion:

Before the procedure you give a single dose of:

Doxycycline 100 mg orally 

For abortion as prophylactic, give 100 mg twice a day orally for seven days

# Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to any tetracycline antibiotic

Pregnancy

Breastfeeding

# Adverse reactions

Common side effects include headache, nausea, vomiting, skin being sensitive to sunlight.

Serious side effects include diarrhoea, joint or muscle pain, bruising or bleeding, severe headaches, vomiting and problems with vision (signs of intracranial hypertension), severe stomach pain, signs of kidney problems (Such as change in the amount of urine), difficulty or pain when swallowing and blood disorders like haemolytic anaemia or low platelet or white blood cell counts.

In rare cases, Doxycycline can cause a serious allergic reaction such as Stevens-Johnson syndrome.

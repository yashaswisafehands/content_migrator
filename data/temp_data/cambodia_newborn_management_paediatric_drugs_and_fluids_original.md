# Chapter: Paediatric Drugs

### Paediatric Drugs

Drug dose of common drugs for newborns below one month of age. See drug list for indications and side effects.

<color style="#b5093f">**Ampicillin**</color>

Age: Under 7 days

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 2 times per day**

Age: Above 7 days

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 3 times per day**

<color style="#b5093f">**Amoxicillin**</color>

Age: 0-28 days

Administration: Orally

Dose: **40 mg/kg/dose x 2 times per day**

<color style="#b5093f">**Caffeine citrate**</color>

**Loading dose**

Administration: oral/IV slowly over 30 minutes

Dose: **20 mg/kg**

**Maintenance dose after 24 hours**

Administration:	oral/IV slowly over 30 minutes

Dose: **5 mg/kg**

Maintenance dose can be increased by **5 mg/kg** every 24 hours to a maximum of **20 mg/kg/day**, unless side-effects develop. Continue 4-5 days after cessation of apnoea.

<color style="#b5093f">**Cefotaxime**</color>

Weight: below 2.5 kg

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 2 times per day**

Age: under 7 days

Administration: IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 3 times per day**

Age: above 7 days

Administration: IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 4 times per day**

<color style="#b5093f">**Ceftriaxone**</color>

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 2 times per day (only IV)**

OR

**100 mg/kg/dose x once per day**(both IM and IV)

<color style="#b5093f">**Cloxacillin**</color>

Age: under 7 days

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 3 times per day according to national guidelines**

Age: above 7 days

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **50 mg/kg/dose x 3 times per day according to national guidelines**

<color style="#b5093f">**Gentamicin**</color>

Age: Under 7 days

Weight: Below 2.5 kg

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **3-4 mg/kg/dose x once per day**

Age: Under 7 days

Weight: Above 2.5 kg

Administration: IM/IV

Dose: **5 mg/kg/dose x once per day**

Dose: **7.5 mg/kg/dose once per day**

<color style="#b5093f">**Penicillin**</color>

Age: Under 7 days
Administration: IV

Dose: **50.000 IU/kg/dose 2 times per day**

Age: above 7 days

Administration: IV

Dose: **50.000 IU/kg/dose 4 times per day**

<color style="#b5093f">**Phenobarbital**</color>

**Loading Dose**

Administration: IV slowly over 5 minutes/IM/Oral

Dose: **20 mg/kg/dose once per day**

Further dose: **10 mg/kg/dose if convusion does not stop 30 min after loading dose.**

Total maximum including loading dose **40 mg/kg.**

**Maintenance Dose**

Administration: Oral

Dose: **5 mg/kg/dose once per day**
**Note: Do NOT give Diazepam for managin convulsion.**

# Chapter: Fluid Management

### Fluid management of newborns

The total daily volume of fluids (ml/kg/day) required by a newborn is determined by weight and day of life.

The total daily volume of fluids is spread over 24 hours. It is composed of either enteral only, intravenous only or a combination of the two.

The maximum fluid volume in intravenous fluids for a newborn is 150 ml/kg/day.

The usual maximum oral intake for a newborn is 180 ml/kg/day.

The reference weight is the birthweight until the birth weight is exceeded.

Total daily fluid requirements in ml/kg/day (accumulated oral, nasogastric tube, and IV)

If very low birth weight or not able to tolerate oral feeds give IV 10% glucose solution.

Reintroduce expressed breast milk feeds slowly, and gradually increase by 1-2 ml per feed each day.

If IV fluids are given, reduce the rate as the volume of oral or gastric milk feeds increases and when sucking well at the breast and gaining weight reduce the cup or gastric tube feeds.

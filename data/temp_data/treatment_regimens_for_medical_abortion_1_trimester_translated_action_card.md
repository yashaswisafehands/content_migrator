# Chapter: Treatment regimens for medical abortion 1st trimester

## Mifepristone & Misoprostol:

- **First:** mifepristone 200 mg orally, single dose 1-2 days before misoprostol administration. 
- **Second:** misoprostol 800 mcg sublingually.

![Image](7cc745f69087461381a744e5d76840c0)

## Misoprostol only:

- 800 mcg sublingually every 3-12 hours.

![Image](0e66184ea5984de7a1be5bb1262ceb44)

## <12 weeks:

Following provision of Mifepristone at a health care facility, women may take Misoprostol at home. This is safe and usually more private and convenient.

## Missed abortion

Missed abortion is when a pregnancy stops developing, where the embryo/fetus/embryonic tissue or empty gestation sac remains in the uterus and the cervical os is closed.

Symptoms may include pain and bleeding. Some do not experience any symptoms at all.

If available, an ultrasound scan can indicate/diagnose a missed abortion by showing an embryo or foetus without cardiac activity, or what appears to be an early developing pregnancy with only a fluid-filled sac being visible within the uterus.

![Image](3954531f9c8743e1b5fb06b32bde3806)

Medical surgical (vacuum aspiration) and expectant management are all options for management of missed abortion.

**For medical management of missed abortion <14 weeks, the combination of mifepristone & misoprostol is recommended over misoprostol alone.**

**Recommended regimen:**

Mifepristone 200 mg orally, followed by misoprostol 800 mcg sublingually or vaginally 1-2 days after mifepristone.

**Alternative regimen:**

Misoprostol 800 mcg sublingually.

## Self-management approaches for medical abortion

![Image](ab510ed709df4cc797be72cb94d9ca12)

Women may self-manage parts or all of the abortion process for a variety of reasons related to individual circumstances and preferences.

For medical abortion in the 1st trimester, women should be offered to self-manage any of or all three parts of the medical abortion process (using the combination of mifepristone plus misoprostol or using misoprostol alone):

1. Self-assessment of eligibility (determining pregnancy duration; ruling out contraindications).
2. Self-administration of abortion medicines outside of a health-care facility and without the direct supervision of a trained health worker, and management of the abortion process. 
3. Self-assessment of the success of the abortion.

# Chapter: Emergency Referral – Manual Removal of Placenta

**Do not refer if emergency is not controlled!**

**Actions to be taken before referral**

The general steps to be taken before referring the woman to an FRU, are as follows:

- Evaluate general condition of woman and look for signs of shock.
- Monitor the vital signs and bleeding every 15 minutes.
- Massage the uterus and continue oxytocin infusion.
- If MRP is not possible in your facility, ensure to stabilize the woman, control bleeding and give first dose of broad-spectrum antibiotics before referral.
- Do not give the woman anything to eat or drink since she may require an obstetric intervention under anaesthesia.
- Raise the foot end of the bed so that her head is lower than her body. This will help increase the flow of blood to the heart and vital organs.
- Keep the woman warm and covered with a blanket. If she is in shock, she might feel cold even in warm weather. 
- Ensure that family members/attendants accompany the woman to the FRU. Health care provider should also accompany her. 
- Arrange for two or three blood donors in case a blood transfusion is required.  
- Send the referral note and inform the referral facility about woman’s condition, interventions performed and expected procedures to be done.
- On the way to the FRU, estimate the amount of blood lost (by counting the number of pads soiled).

## Essential emergency drugs:

- **Oxytocin, Misoprostol**and**Ergometrine**for treatment of post-partum haemorrhage.
- **Ampicillin, Gentamicin**and**Metronidazole** for infections.
- **Diazepam**for pain relief.

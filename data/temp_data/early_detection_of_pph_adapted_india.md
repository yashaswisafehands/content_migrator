# Chapter: Assessment of postpartum blood loss

Visual blood loss estimation is often inaccurate and underestimated. To improve detection and treatment of PPH. All woman giving birth should receive routine measurement of postpartum blood loss.

Use a **calibrated drape,** if available:****

![Image](8ed7ed1c488e4f10880c362a78b46b4f)

Or use gravimetric technique by weighing the blood loss on a scale:

Remember to subtract the weight of the material used to collect the blood.

Accurate assessment of blood after childbirth is important to detect and manage PPH early, as vital signs may remain normal until blood loss reaches upto 1000 ml.

The images below provide an approximate visual representation of the amount of blood loss in each scenario:

Remember to subtract the weight of the bloody material.

**Observe** blood loss, blood flow, uterine tone, vital signs and general condition every 30 minutes and blood pressure and pulse for initial two hours after birth. Document the findings on case sheet.

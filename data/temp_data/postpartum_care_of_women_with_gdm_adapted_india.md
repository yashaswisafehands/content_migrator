# Chapter: Postpartum care of women with GDM

## Postpartum care of women with GDM

- Encourage and support early breast feeding. 
- Offer safe and reversible postpartum contraception to space or limit her next pregnancy 
- Women with GDM are at high risk of developing Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in the future.
- Maternal glucose levels usually return to normal after delivery.
- Encourage follow up visit and perform 75 gm OGTT at 6 weeks postpartum to evaluate glycaemic status.

Cut off values for diagnosis are:

- Fasting blood sugar: ≥126 mg/dL
- 2-hour blood sugar after 75 g of oral glucose:
- Normal: <140 mg/dL
- IGT (impaired glucose tolerance): 140-199 mg/dL
- Diabetes: ≥200 mg/dL

If the test is normal, counsel about lifestyle modifications, weight monitoring, diet & exercise.

Advise to get annual screening for diabetes in a Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) clinic as per protocols.

If the test is positive or she has IGT, link her with national NCD program for further management.

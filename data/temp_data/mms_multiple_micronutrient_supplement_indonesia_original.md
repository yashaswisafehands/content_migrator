# Chapter: Indications and Usage

MMS-is recommended to improve maternal nutrition, reduce the risk of low birth weight, and prevent anemia during pregnancy. 
MMS can help meet the increased nutritional demands during lactation and support both maternal and infant health

# Dosage and Administration

1 tablet of per day for at least 6 months throughout pregnancy

# Contraindications

Allergy to any of the content of Vitamin A, Vitamin D, Vitamin E, Vitamin C, Thiamin, Riboflavin, Niacin, Vitamin B6, Folic Acid, Vitamin B12, Copper, Iodine, Iron Celenium, Zinc.

# Adverse Reactions

Nausea, vomiting, diarhoea, constipation, headache, bitter taste to the tongue

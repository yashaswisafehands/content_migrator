# Chapter: Pelvic floor muscle exercises

## Urinary stress incontinence:

If the woman is experiencing urinary stress incontinence after childbirth, inform the woman about the benefit of pelvic floor muscle exercises (PFMEs), also known as Kegel exercise, to strengthen the muscles around the bladder, vagina and anus. It can stop incontinence and improve prolapse.

Instruct the woman to do PFME / Kegel exercises  while lying down, sitting or standing.  

Tighten the pelvic floor muscles (as if you’re trying to stop gas or urine). Avoid squeezing your stomach, buttocks, or thighs. 

Recommend that the woman do 20 squeezes 3 times a day. 

Daily practice brings the best results over time.

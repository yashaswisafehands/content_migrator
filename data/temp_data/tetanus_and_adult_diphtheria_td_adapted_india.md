## Indication and Usage

Tetanus and adult diphtheria (Td) vaccine is a combination of tetanus toxoid and diphtheria antigen. 

Recommended to all pregnant women to protect against maternal and neonatal tetanus and diphtheria

## Dosage and Administration

1 dose = 0.5ml intramuscularly in the Upper Arm

If not immunized previously or immunization status is unknown:

Td-1 : early in pregnancy or at first ANC visit
Td-2 : 4 weeks after Td-1

If pregnancy occur within 3 years of last
pregnancy and 2 Td doses were received, give 1 td booster.

## Contraindications

None.

## Adverse Reactions

None.

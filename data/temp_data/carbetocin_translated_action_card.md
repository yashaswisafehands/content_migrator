# Chapter: Carbetocin (Heat-Stable)

## Indication and usage

Heat-stable carbetocin (HSC) is a long-acting synthetic analogue of oxytocin with similar uterotonic effects.

HSC injection is indicated for producing uterine contractions during the third stage of labour to prevent excessive bleeding and is used as an alternative to oxytocin.

> REMEMBER: HSC is NOT recommended for labour induction or labour augmentation (it should never be administered before birth) or for the treatment of postpartum haemorrhage.

**Storage**: HSC does not require refrigeration (as opposed to oxytocin)

## Dosage and administration

For the prevention of PPH only:

Carbetocin 100 micrograms IM/IV within 1 minute of birth. 

IV administration must be slow - never as a fast bolus.

## Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to carbetocin (or oxytocin).

Postpartum haemorrhage

## Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are cardiac arrhythmia, nausea, and vomiting.

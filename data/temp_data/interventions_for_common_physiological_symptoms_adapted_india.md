# Chapter: Lower back and pelvic pain

### Lower back and pelvic pain

![Image](adca0d73bb4240b4982c924c075c2bc8)

- Regular exercise help to prevent back and pelvic pain.
- Teach body posture and keeping the back straight while sitting and lifting objects.
- Avoid sitting cross-legged.
- Avoid heavy lifting and wear flat shoes.

# Chapter: Constipation

### Constipation

- Ensure fibre rich diet, intake of leafy vegetables, and plenty of water   
- If not relieved, give her Isabgol (2 tablespoons to be taken at bedtime, with water or milk).
- Do not prescribe strong laxatives as they may start uterine contractions.

# Chapter: Heartburn

### Heartburn

![Image](7e72ce9f8eb6410099603b9e405d6b6c)

- Diet – avoid spicy and oily foods.
- Recommend to drink cold milk during heart burn episodes.
- Avoid smoking and drinks containing caffeine.
- Sleep with more pillows to raise head. If that does not help, try antacid preparations.

# Chapter: Nausea and vomiting

### Nausea and vomiting

![Image](ac613ed6862b4a0592e7e0eb6cd5916a)

- Recommend gIinger, chamomile and/or vitamin B6.
- Advise on small and frequent meals and avoid greasy food.
- Ensure intake of green vegetables and plenty of fluids.
- If vomiting is excessive in the morning, ask her to eat dry foods, such as roti/paratha, biscuits, or toast, after waking up.
- Women who experience distressing symptoms can receive pharmacological treatment

# Chapter: Varicose veins and oedema

### Varicose veins and oedema

![Image](2c2e0df2f80647c1ac860a37ac27312a)

- Avoid standing for long periods and sitting with the legs crossed.
- Elevate the legs while sitting and lying down. 
- Compression stockings, foot exercises, and water immersion can be used based on the woman’s preferences.

# Chapter: Naso- and Orogastric Tube - Usage

A nasogastric (NG) or orogastric (OG) tube provides access to the stomach and its contents and it can also be used for enteral feeding of infants.

NG/OG tubes can also be used for enteral feeding of infants.

It also enables you to drain gastric contents, decompress the stomach, obtain a specimen of the gastric contents, or introduce a passage into the GI tract.

All necessary equipment should be prepared, assembled and available at the table prior to starting the naso- or orogastric tube.

Equipment and Supplies:

- Personal protective equipment
- OG or NG tube 5 to 6 French
- 2-5 mL syringe for aspiration
- Sterile 10 ml or 20 ml syringe for feeding
- Catheter tip irrigation 60 ml syringe
- Normal saline
- Scissor
- Kidney dish or bowl 
- Adhesive tape
- Paediatric Stethoscope

# Chapter: Naso- and Orogastric tube - Practical information

## Insertion of a naso- or orogastric tube:

- Take Take 5- 6 Fr catheter depending on the gestation and weight of the baby.
- For OG Tube: Measure length from angle of mouth to tragus to midpoint between umbilicus and xiphisternum.
- For NG tube: Measure length from the tip of nose to tragus to midpoint between umbilicus and xiphisternum.
- Insert the tube from mouth/nose till the desired length has been introduced
- Check position using a syringe & a stethoscope to auscultate the gush of air
- Tape the tube to the side of mouth, and close outer end after removing the syringe
- To instil feed-Take a 10 ml syringe barrel without the plunger.Insert nozzle into the open end of the feeding tube.Pour milk into the syringe and wait for it to go down slowly by gravity.
- After a feed, close the open end
- Place newborn in right lateral position for 15 to 20 minutes to avoid regurgitation
- No need to burp a gavage-fed baby.
- Check abdominal girth at next feeding session & proceed to feed, if no increase in girth.
- If the girth increases by 2 cm;do a pre-feed gastric aspirate and analyse the amount and content to decide about continuing /discontinuing feeds.
- While pulling out a feeding tube, it must be kept pinched and pulled out gently.
- Always confirm the position of the tube prior to giving a feed.
- <color style="blue">**Routine pre-feed gastric aspirates are not recommended.**</color>

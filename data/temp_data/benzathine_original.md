# Chapter: Benzathine

## Indication and usage

Benzathine penicillin is used to treat Treponema pallidum infection also known as Syphilis.

## Dosage and administration

**Early syphilis:** Benzathine penicillin 1.8 mg (2.4 million units) single dose. 

**Late latent syphilis:** Benzathine penicillin 1.8 g (2.4 million units) IM once weekly for 3 executive weeks. 

Administer 900 mg (1.2 million units) in each buttock.

The interval between consecutive doses of benzathine penicillin should not exceed 14 days. 

Should never be administered as IV injection/infusion.

## Contraindications

Allergy to penicillin.

## Adverse reactions

Common: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, itching, sweating, allergic reaction, flushing, anxiety, headache, muscle/joint pain 

Uncommon: breathing difficulties, fever, seizure, rash/itching. 

A serious allergic reaction to this drug is rare.

# Chapter: Indications and usage

Nicardipine is used to treat hypertension and angina (chest pain). Nicardipine is in a class of medications called calcium channel blockers.

**Warning**: Rapid pharmacologic reductions in blood pressure may produce systemic hypotension and reflex tachycardia. If either occurs with nicardipine, consider decreasing the dose by half or stopping the infusion.

Bolus administration or intravenous administration not controlled by the use of an electronic syringe driver or a volumetric pump is NOT recommended and can increase the risk of serious hypotension.

Due to the possible risk of pulmonary oedema or excessive decrease in blood pressure, caution should be taken if magnesium sulphate is used concomitantly.

## Tocolysis:

The use of nicardipine for severe pre-eclampsia during the third trimester of pregnancy could potentially produce an undesirable tocolytic effect which could potentially interfere with the spontaneous induction of labour.

# Dosage and Administration

For severe hypertension in pregnancy:

## Intravenous:

It is recommended to provide a continuous infusion of nicardipine starting at 3 to 5 mg/h, depending on the blood pressure and clinical situation.

After 30 minutes, depending on the effect observed, this rate can be increased or decreased by increments of 0.5 mg/h.

Doses higher than 4mg/h are generally not exceeded in the treatment of pre-eclampsia, however the rate should not exceed 15 mg/h.

The speed can be increased in steps of 0.5 or 1 mg every 15 minutes, not to exceed 15 mg/h.

Oral:

Initial dose: 20 mg orally 3 times a day.

Dose d'entretien : 20 à 40 mg par voie orale, 3 fois par jour.

Comments: Dose may be increased using intervals of at least 3 days.

# Contraindications

Nicardipine should not be used if:

- You have severe narrowing of the aortic valve in your heart (aortic stenosis)

- Compensatory hypertension, i.e. in case of an arteriovenous shunt or aortic coarctation

- Unstable angina

- Within 8 days after myocardial infarction

- Patients with rare hereditary problems of fructose intolerance should not take this medicine

- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients

- Nicardipine should be used with caution in patients with suspected coronary ischemia. Occasionally, patients have developed an increased frequency, duration, or severity of angina upon starting or increasing nicardipine dosage, or during the course of treatment.

# Adverse reactions

Common side effects may include:

- headache, dizziness;

- swelling in your feet;

- weakness;

- flushing (warmth, redness, or tingly feeling); or

- nausea

## Indications and Usage

Reduces the risk of low birth weight, puerperal sepsis, maternal anaemia, iron deficiency, and neural tube defects.

Recommended for all pregnant women.

## Dosage

**Routine antenatal and postnatal supplementation:**

1 tablet of IFA (containing 60 mg elemental iron and 500 mcg folic acid) per day orally after 12 weeks of gestation to 6 months of postpartum.

**Treatment of mild and moderate iron deficiency anaemia :**

Two tablets of IFA (containing 60 mg elemental Iron and 500 mcg Folic Acid) daily, orally.

Continue with the prophylactic IFA dose, if haemoglobin levels have come up to normal level.

## Contraindications

**Folic acid:**

Doses above 0.4mg daily should not be given without concomitant administration of vitamin B12, unless vitamin B12 deficiency can be ruled out.

**Iron:**

- Hypersensitivity
- Hemosiderosis –excessive accumulation of iron in the tissues 
- Hemochromatosis -overload of iron in the body
- Haemolytic anaemia

## Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are nausea, abdominal pains, diarrhoea and constipation. Stool becomes black and dusty.

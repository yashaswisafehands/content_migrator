# Chapter: Septrin (Co-trimoxazole)

## Indication and usage

Septrin (Cotrimoxazole) is a combination of two different antibiotics: sulfamethoxazole and trimethoprima used to treat infection.

## Dosage and Administration

Oral mg 
2 x 80 mg/400 mg Tablets for 5 days

## Contraindications

Allergic to sulfamethoxazole, trimethoprim or co-trimoxazole.

Allergic to sulphonamide medicines
Severe liver or severe kidney problems.
Thrombocytopenia

Thrombocytopenia

Should not be given to infants during the first 6 weeks of life.

## Adverse reactions

Common: headache, nausea, diarrhoea, and skin rash.
Uncommon: fever, muscle or joint ache, blistering of the skin, mouth ulcers, swelling of the eyes, lips, mouth or face, breathing difficulties, yellowing of the skin or eye, dark urine, pale stools

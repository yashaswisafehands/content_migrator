## Introduction

Emergency contraception (EC) can prevent pregnancy after unprotected sex, following contraceptive misuse or failure (split condom or missed pill), rape or coerced sex by preventing or delaying Ovulation. It also thickens cervical mucus results in trapping of sperms, and inhibition of fertilization.

EC pills prevents pregnancy when taken up to 3 days (72 hours) after unprotected sex.

## Medications (from highest to lowest effectiveness)

- Copper IUD for EC 
- EC pills contain only progestin – Levonorgestrel (1.5 mg per tablet)

## Common side-effects

Some may experience headaches, abdominal pain, nausea and vomiting, dizziness, fatigue, breast tenderness, next menstrual bleeding - early or delayed.

## Contraindication

It is not consumed in pregnancy.

EC pill safe with all medical conditions.

### Shake and shout

- Assess level of consciousness and reflexes.

### Call a CAB – initial assessment

> Call for help!

- **C**irculation – take pulse and blood pressure
- **A**irway – ensure it is open
- **B**reathing – take respiratory rate, listen for pulmonary oedema, give **oxygen<**color style="#b5093f"> </color>with a mask

### Prevent fits

- Start IV line with **Ringer's lactate**, run at 100mls/min 
- Put **4 gm Magnesium Sulphate** into **200 ml normal saline<**color style="#b5093f"> </color>and run in as a side drip over 20 minutes 
- Give **5 gm Magnesium Sulphate** IM in each buttock, with **1 ml Lignocaine** per injection 
- Assess reflexes, respiratory rate and urine output before repeating **5 gm** IM **Magnesium Sulphate 4 hourly**

### Call a CAB – initial response

**Circulation**

- Take blood pressure and pulse every 15 minutes
- If BP 160 mmHg or more OR diastolic 110 mmHg or more after the **Magnesium Sulphate** has been started
- Give<color style="#b5093f"> **</color>10 Nifedipine**orally
- If the pulse is greater than 110 bpm and **Labetalol<**color style="#b5093f"> </color>is availab**le give Labetalol**
- Continue giving **Nifedipine** or **Labetalol** every 15 minutes until Systolic blood pressure is less than 160 mmHg AND diastolic blood pressure less than 110 mmHg

**Airway**

- Ensure airway is open

**Breathing**

- Give **Oxygen**<color style="#b5093f"> </color>via facemask

### Secondary survey

## Big 5

1. CNS – AVPU, reflexes
2. CVS – BP & P every 15 minutes until stable and then every hour
3. Respiratory system – monitor respiratory rate, **oxygen<**color style="#b5093f"> </color>saturation
4. Liver and GIT – look for jaundice and take blood for AST
5. Renal - Catheterise, test urine, do hourly urine output; send blood for urea and creatinine

## Forgotten 4

1. Haematological system – take blood for haemoglobin and platelet count
2. Endocrine system– measure blood glucose, assess breasts and thyroid
3. Immunological system – HIV-status + TP 4 hourly
4. Musculoskeletal system – assess oedema and examine for DVTs

## Core 1

- Determine gestational age
- Estimate foetal weight, and amniotic fluid
- Assess if foetus is alive or not
- Assess cervical ripeness

## Further management

Continue monitoring the blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, urine output, (O2 saturation if available) hourly.

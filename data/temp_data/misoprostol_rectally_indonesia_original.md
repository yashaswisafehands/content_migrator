# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Misoprostol is a synthetic prostaglandin E1.
Misoprostol is indicated to produce uterine contractions during the third stage of labour to prevent and control postpartum bleeding or haemorrhage.

# Dosage and Administration

## For prevention of postpartum haemorrhage as an alternative to Oxytocin:

400-600 mcg rectally.

## For treatment of Uterine Atony:

600-1000 mcg rectally

## For treatment of postpartum haemorrhage:

600-1000 mcg rectally. 
Maximum dose: 1600 mcg.

# Contraindications

Prostaglandin allergy.
Prostaglandins should not be given to pregnant women as they cause abortion.

# Adverse reactions

One out of three treated with misoprostol will have diarrhea or abdominal pain.

It is also common to see a non-allergic rash. 

A few percent will experience a transient moderate increase in temperature.

Some will have nausea, vomiting, constipation or flatulence. 

Less common headache and dizziness is experienced. 

Rarely misoprostol can cause liver affection.

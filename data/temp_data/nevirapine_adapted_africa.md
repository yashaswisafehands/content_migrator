# Chapter: Indication & Usage

Nevirapine (NVP) is a non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NNRTI) widely used in HIV-1 management as a part of combination ART regimens in adults, adolescents, and infants >15 days old.

Also used for Preventing parent to child transmission (PPTCT): Neonatal prophylaxis for HIV-exposed newborns initiated at birth.

# Dosage & Administration

## Adults:

- 200 mg once daily for the first 14 days  
- 200 mg twice daily thereafter (maintenance dose).

## Newborns:

- >2500 g: 15 mg (1.5 mL syrup) once daily × 6 weeks.
- 2000–2500 g: 10 mg (1 mL syrup) once daily × 6 weeks.
- <2000 g: 2 mg/kg (0.2 mL/kg) once daily × 6 weeks

> (Extend to 12 weeks if maternal ART <24 weeks by delivery)

# Contraindications

Known hypersensitivity to nevirapine or NNRTIs.

Caution in non-occupational post-exposure prophylaxis due to liver toxicity

Pregnant woman with high CD4 counts as there is risk of life threatening hepatoxicity.

# Adverse Effects

Hepatotoxicity

Skin reactions: Rashes (majority mild/moderate); rarely severe (Stevens-Johnson syndrome/toxic epidermal necrolysis); monitor closely first 6 weeks.

Other side effects include fever, fatigue, muscle/joint pain, and hypersensitivity (e.g. facial swelling, conjunctivitis).

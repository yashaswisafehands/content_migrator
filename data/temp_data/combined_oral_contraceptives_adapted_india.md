## Introduction

Combined oral contraceptives (COCs) contain low doses of two synthetic hormones progestin and an oestrogen It prevents ovulation by suppressing follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH),prevents implantation,  thicken cervical mucus, makes it difficult for sperm to pass through.

One pill is to be taken every day without any break After a pack of 28 pills is over, start another pack from next day.

## Medication examples

Mala-N

- Mala N contains Levonorgestrel (0.15 mg) and Ethinyl estradiol (30 mcg)
- Each strip of Mala-N contains 21 hormonal  and 7 Nonhormonal (iron) tablets.

## Common side-effects

Irregular and unexpected bleeding, no monthly bleeding, headaches, nausea, dizziness, breast tenderness, weight gain, mood changes, changes in sex drive, and acne.

## Contraindications

Breastfeeding women<  6 months  and non-breast feeding <3 month postpartum, woman with hypertension (BP 140/90 mmHg or higher), Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT), heart disease, diabetes with vascular complications, recurrent migraines, unexplained vaginal bleeding, breast cancer, liver disease and those currently taking anticonvulsants or Rifampicin for tuberculosis.

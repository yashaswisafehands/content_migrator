# Chapter: Pre-conception counselling for diabetic women

## Pre-conception counselling for diabetic women

Maintenance of a good blood glucose level before conception reduces the risk of complications such as miscarriage, congenital malformation, stillbirth, and neonatal death.

Counsel the women on BMI and blood sugar estimation before the next pregnancy

Desired blood sugar levels are:

- FBS - <100 mg/dL
- 2 hr PPBS - <140 mg/dL

Advice to consult a doctor as soon as she misses her period, for screening and advice for appropriate management

## Counselling tips

- GDM can be easily controlled by diet (MNT) and exercise. 
- Only a few women, whose blood sugar is not controlled by diet and physical exercise, require oral antidiabetic (metformin) or insulin injections. 
- GDM can be treated with oral metformin tablets as they do not harm the foetus.
- Insulin injections are required only during pregnancy. 
- Insulin will be stopped in most of the cases after pregnancy.
- Injecting insulin over the abdomen is 100% safe and it cannot reach the foetus.
- Modification of diet is easy and will not cost more. Sweets should be always avoided during pregnancy.
- If the blood sugar is not properly monitored, it may harm the mother and the baby. 
- If taking insulin, always keep sugar, as hypoglycemia can occur at any time. 
- Pregnant women with GDM should deliver at health facilities.

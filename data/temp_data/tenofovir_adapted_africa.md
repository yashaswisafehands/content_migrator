# Chapter: Indication and Usage

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (TDF) is a nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NtRTI) used widely in the treatment of HIV-1. It is a first-line ART in combination with other antiretrovirals for infected adults, adolescents, and children (weighing ≥35 kg).

Helps in prevention of parent-to-child transmission (PPTCT) as it is a part of ART regimen for pregnant woman.

It is also a drug of choice for chronic hepatitis B treatment in pregnancy from 28 weeks of gestation and given as Hep B prophylaxis to woman.

# Dosage and Administration

300 mg orally once daily as part of a fixed-dose combination in ART therapy.

Same dose is used for Hepatitis B prophylaxis in pregnant women after 28 weeks of gestation.

**Renal adjustment:**Dose modification required in patients with renal impairment.

# Contraindications

Known hypersensitivity to Tenofovir or formulation components.

Use with caution in patients with pre-existing renal dysfunction.

Avoid concurrent use with other nephrotoxic agents unless necessary.

# Adverse Effects

Common side effects include gastrointestinal symptoms like nausea and vomiting, and gradual loss of bone mineral density, which may lead to osteopenia or osteoporosis over time.

Severe side effects may involve renal toxicity, hepatomegaly with steatosis, and rare but serious lactic acidosis.

# Chapter: Indications and Usage

For the preparation of dilatation and evacuation up to 16 weeks pregnancy.

# Dosage and Administration

Mifepristone 200 mg orally, 24 to 48 hours before administration of misoprostol regimen.

# Contraindications

Ectopic pregnancy

# Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are heavy bleeding, abdominal pain, uterine cramping, nausea, vomiting, and diarrhoea.

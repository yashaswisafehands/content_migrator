# Chapter: Essential Steps for AMTSL

### Essential steps to prevent post partum haemorrhage

Active management of the third stage of labour (AMTSL) helps prevent postpartum haemorrhage.

The most essential steps for active management of third stage of labour are:

**1.  Uterotonic drug -**Administration of one of the following uterotonic drugs within one minute after the delivery:

- **Oxytocin** 10 IU IM/IV

Or

- **Heat-stable Carbetocin**100 microgram IM/IV.

Or

- **Misoprostol**600 microgram orally.

![Image](ed8bed8a74054546a35bdda71827b087)

<color style="blue">Note:  Before administering uterotonics rule out the present of 2nd baby in uterus by palpating the abdomen.</color>

**2.	Controlled cord traction**-Delivery of the placenta using controlled cord traction with counter-traction above the pubic bone. Ensure that placenta is delivered complete with all membranes. It helps to reduce chances of a retained placenta and subsequent bleeding i.e.  PPH

![Image](a726a1082a7b43bbb2006c85bb78944a)

**3.	Uterine massage-**
After delivery  of the placenta massage the fundus of the uterus until it is well contracted, thus prevents atonic PPH

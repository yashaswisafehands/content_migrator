# Chapter: Indications and usage

- Prophylaxis against pertussis, diphtheria and tetanus
- Prophylaxis against Hepatitis B
- Prophylaxis against purulent-septic diseases caused by Haemophilus influenzae.

## Dosage and usage

**Administer 0.5 ml intramuscularly into the anterolateral surface of the thigh.**

- 1st dose at 2 months
- 2nd dose at 3 months
- 3rd dose at 4 months

# DTP containing vaccine

## Indications and usage

Prevention of diphtheria, Tetanus, and Pertussis. The vaccine is administered as part of the Pentovaccines.

## Dosage and Administration

**Administer 0.5 ml intramuscularly into the anterolateral aspect of the thigh.**

- 1st dose: At  2 months
- 2nd dose: At 3 months
- 3rd dose: At 4 months
- Booster dose: At 16-23 months 
- DT booster: At 6 years
- Td booster: At 16,26,36,45,56 years

## Consideration

Vaccines should be stored at 2-8°C. It should not be frozen.

## Contraindications

- A severe allergic reaction (life-threatening) after a previous dose
- A severe allergy (life-threatening) to any vaccine component 
- Newborns who are mildly ill can receive the vaccine. Newborns who are moderately or severely ill should wait until they recover unless the risk outweighs the benefits.

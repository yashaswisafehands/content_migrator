# Chapter: Anaemia

### Significant Symptoms of Antenatal Complications

Identify significant symptoms of antenatal complications and offer treatment or referral as appropriate.

## Anaemia

![Image](33420553a2794832811b3253b9796314)

- Paleness on face, nails, tongue and conjunctiva of eyes and palms, dizziness, weakness, tiredness, shortness of breath, and palpitations.

See Practical Procedure 'Routine Maternal Screening Tests’

# Chapter: Antepartum Haemmorhage

### Significant Symptoms of Antenatal Complications

Identify significant symptoms of antenatal complications and offer treatment or referral as appropriate.

## Antepartum Haemorrhage

![Image](5074822321a3413da5d7ba557fb612ea)

- Vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain, uterine contractions and tenderness, irritable uterus, increased baseline tone, and evidence of foetal distress.

# Chapter: Urinary Tract Infection 

### Significant Symptoms of Antenatal Complications

Identify significant symptoms of antenatal complications and offer treatment or referral as appropriate.

## Urinary Tract Infection

![Image](287378e2f0a24b83b7cd859dee9d7b78)

- Burning sensation when passing urine, frequent urination, cloudy dark or strong-smelling urine, blood in urine, tenderness at the sides of the abdomen and back, and hyperthermia.

# Chapter: Premature/Prolonged Rupture of Membranes 

### Significant Symptoms of Antenatal Complications

Identify significant symptoms of antenatal complications and offer treatment or referral as appropriate.

## Premature/Prolonged rupture of membranes

![Image](0419eb8235cd47a08cd587e87623814d)

- Leakage or sudden gush of fluid from the vagina, fever, heavy or foul-smelling vaginal discharge, abdominal pain, foetal tachycardia.

# Chapter: Venous Thromboembolism

### Significant Symptoms of Antenatal Complications

Identify significant symptoms of antenatal complications and offer treatment or referral as appropriate.

## Venous Thromboembolism

![Image](3ca6d31121b04e6c9fecd184e16a4537)

# Chapter: Usage 

## Urinary Catheter

Emptying the bladder with urinary catheter is indicated:

1. If a women in labour fails to urinate spontanously and progress of labour is dissatisfactory, **OR**
2. Prior to applying vacuum extraction, **OR**
3. In case of postpartum haemorrhage, **AND**
4. In any severely ill patient with for example sepsis or severe preeclampsia to monitor the urine output. In this case, the catheter should stay in place until the condition improves.

# Chapter: Practical Information 

## Practical Information

The urine should be clear, if not, there is a urine tract infection and antibiotics should be administered.

All patients in a critical state (sepsis, severe preeclampsia, eclampsia, bleeding, shock) must have a permanent catheter, and the hourly urine output should be at least 30 ml.

![Image](59ff8b215b4e4050a87db9139a1ea338)

If less, the underlying cause should be treated and fluid replacement considered, although not more than 80 ml per hour (oral and IV) for women with preeclampsia as the risk of fluid overload and pulmonary oedema is increased.

Before applying a permanent catheter, the area around the urethra should be cleaned twice with sterile gauze swabs and normal saline water. Use sterile technique to apply urinary catheter.

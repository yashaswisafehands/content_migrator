# Chapter: Misoprostol only regime (1. trimester)

# Indication and usage

Misoprostol is a prostaglandin E1 analogue that can be used either in combination with Mifepristone or on its own to induce abortion or manage incomplete abortion.

# Dosage and Administration

## Misoprostol only:

Misoprostol 800 mg vaginally or sublingually until successful achievement of complete abortion.

# Contraindications

Previous allergic reaction to misoprostol

Known or suspected ectopic pregnancy

# Precautions

IUD in place

Serious/unstable health problems, including but not limited to hemorrhagic disorders, heart disease and severe anemia

# Adverse reactions:

1 in 3 treated with misoprostol will have diarrhoea or abdominal pain.

It is also common to see a non-allergic rash

A few will experience a transient moderate increase in temperature.

Some will have nausea, vomiting, constipation or flatulence.

Less common: headaches and dizziness is experienced.

Rarely Misoprostol can cause liver damage.

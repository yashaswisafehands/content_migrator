# Chapter: Essential Steps for AMTSL

### Essential steps to prevent postpartum haemorrhage

Active management of the third stage of labour (AMTSL) helps prevent postpartum haemorrhage.

The most important intervention to in reducinge postpartum haemorrhage is:

**Administration of one of the following uterotonic drugs within one minute after the delivery:**

- **Oxytocin** 10 IU IM/IV

Or

- **Heat-stable Carbetocin**100 microgram IM/IV.

Or

- **Misoprostol**600 microgram orally.

![Image](652a5a0e658e4e29a7c4ec5890e9d2d6)

AMTSL may also include:

Delivery of the placenta using controlled cord traction with counter-traction above the pubic bone ONLY if a skilled birth attendant is present.

![Image](e9276c90919f4893b0dcbcf4a6d0b6f0)

Palpation of the uterus after delivery of the placenta to assess firmness. If soft, massage the uterus.

# Chapter: Risk factors

> The biggest predictor is having a pre-existing history of a mental health condition or a strong family history of significant mental health conditions.

Significant risk factors include:

- **Domestic/sexual and gender-based violence**
- **Lack of sleep**
- **Unintended pregnancy**(result of rape, lack of modern contraception, forced/ early marriage)
- **Women in conflict areas** (state of emergency/ natural disasters, migrants with language and cultural barriers)
- **Lack of social or practical support, financial resources, and access to quality health care**
- **Previous stillbirth, repeated miscarriage**. Globally and in India approximately one in four pregnancies will end in a miscarriage. Further, globally, 2.6 million stillbirths occur annually, while in India, the number is 0.34 million. Perinatal loss is a significant risk factor for women in developing mental health conditions.​
- **Traumatic births.** Some women experience birth or pregnancy as traumatic, especially those with a history of childhood sexual abuse. This can lead to Post Traumatic Stress Disorder(PTSD)
- **Poor social determinants of health**
- **Early or forced marriage**
- **Teenage pregnancy and single mothers**
- **Unemployment**
- **Low levels of education**
- **High domestic workloads**
- **Difficulties with breastfeeding**
- **Fertility difficulties**
- **Substance abuse**
- **Poor nutrition**

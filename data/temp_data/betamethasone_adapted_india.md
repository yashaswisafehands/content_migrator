# Chapter: Indications and Usage

For lung maturation and prevention of respiratory distress of the newborn in preterm labour between 24 to 34 weeks gestation.

For conditions that lead to imminent preterm delivery: 

- Antepartum haemorrhage
- Preterm premature rupture of membrane
- Severe pre-eclampsia/Eclampsia

# Dosage and Administration

12 mg IM, two doses, 24 hours apart (24 mg in total)

# Contraindications

Active untreated infections.

Frank chorioamnionitis 

Following signs and symptoms in the woman suggest Frank chorioamnionitis.

History of fever and lower abdominal pain

Foul smelling vaginal discharge, tachycardia, and uterine tenderness

Foetal tachycardia

# Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are mood changes and other mental disturbances, shortness of breath, oedema and diabetic metabolism.

# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Prevents bleeding complications in the newborn.

# Dosage and Administration

1 mg for newborns weighing 1000 gm or more.

0.5 mg for newborns weighing less than 1000 grams.

Administer Vitamin K1 intramuscularly immediately after delivery but within 24 hours.

# Contraindications

None.

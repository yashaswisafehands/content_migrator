# Chapter: Special ANC for women with GDM 

## Antenatal care for women with GDM

- Antenatal care should be provided by a gynaecologist if available.
- A foetal examination should be performed by ultrasound at 18-20 weeks, if GDM is diagnosed before 20 weeks of gestation.
- A foetal growth scan should be performed at 28-30 weeks and 34-36 weeks of gestation with at least a 3-week gap in between.

![Image](3ebdebf99a42461f9d7bf2ff69791afe)

- GDM women with a good blood sugar control and no complications should continue ANC as per high-risk pregnancy protocol at least once a month.
- GDM women with an uncontrolled blood sugar level or any other complications, refer to a higher facility for ANC at least once a month.
- The pregnant woman with GDM must visit the PMSMA day for care by specialist. 
- A red sticker should be added onto the MCP cards of all GDM positive women to indicate the high-risk status of the pregnancy.
- Monitor for abnormal foetal growth (macrosomia/growth restriction) and polyhydramnios at each ANC visit by performing abdominal examination.
- Listen to the foetal heart rate at each ANC visit.

![Image](b318fa4aa1cd4445b386258374a023fd)

- Monitor diligently for hypertension, proteinuria, and other obstetric complications.
- If labour is induced or woman goes into preterm labour between 24-34 weeks of gestation administer antenatal steroids - Inj. Dexamethasone 6 mg IM every 12 hours for 2 consecutive days and monitor the blood sugar levels for 72 hours. Adjust insulin dose as required.
- Teach woman the daily foetal activity assessment – ask the woman to lie down on the side after a meal and count the foetal kicks. If less than 10 within 12 hours, immediately consult a health care provider

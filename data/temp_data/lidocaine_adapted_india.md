# Chapter: Indication and usage

Lidocaine/ Lignocaine is a type of local analgesia used for pain relief at deep IM injection with e.g. Magnesium Sulphate and for pain relief prior to suturing or invasive procedures.

Inject the lidocaine in the area where you are going to suture or around the margins to ensure adequate local anaesthesia before beginning the procedure.

# Dosage and administration

## Infiltration

Give 0.5% or 1% subcutaneous and/or intramuscularly. Maximum dosage is 5mg/kg.

1 bottle of 20 ml 1 % Lidocaine is equivalent to 200 mg and should be enough for suturing a perineal tear or other minor invasive procedures.

##

Lidocaine 0.5% or 1% with/without adrenaline percutaneously. 

Maximum dose: Lidocaine 5 mg/kg

## IM Injection

Add 1 ml of 2% lidocaine to the medicine that should be given IM (e.g. Magnesium Sulphate).

Use aseptic technique to prevent infections, and aspirate before injecting to ensure the medicine is not injected directly into a blood vessel.

# Duration of analgesia

The analgesia is efficient 5 minutes after injection 
 
The duration of analgesia is 2 hours.

# Contraindications

Lignocaine is contraindicated in patients with a known history of hypersensitivity to local analgesics.

# Adverse reactions

Common side effects of lignocaine include mild local pain or swelling at the injection site, dizziness, drowsiness, tingling, numbness, nausea, and a metallic taste.

Rare but serious side effects include allergic reactions, seizures, coma, cardiac arrhythmias, nerve damage, and respiratory depression.

At symptoms of overdose, stop treatment, call for help, monitor closely and treat symptoms.

# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Alkaloid analgetic drug for pain relief for the manual removal of placenta

# Dosage and administration

0.25 mg intramuscularly, 15 minutes before the manual removal of placenta.

# Contraindications

It should not be used for people with angle closure glaucoma.

Atropine crosses the placenta and may cause tachycardia in the fetus though any proven increase in the frequency of malformations or other direct or indirect harmful effects on the fetus having been observed.

Therefore, administration should be limited only for the manual removal of placenta.

# Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are nausea, vomiting, a dry mouth, large pupils, urinary retention, constipation, and a fast heart rate

# Chapter: Preparation of 1% Sodium Hypochlorite Solution

1. Wear Personal Protective Equipment
2. Take the bottle of Sodium Hypochlorite Solution 
3. Take 1 Mug full or 1 Liter of Sodium Hypochlorite Solution and put it in a bucket
4. Now Add 9 Mug full or 9 Liters of Water in the bucket with Sodium Hypochlorite Solution 
5. If Sodium Hypochlorite is not available use bleaching powder
6. Take 1 liter of water, take out 30 ML of water from it in a separate mug
7. Add 3 tea spoon full Bleaching powder in 30 ml mug and mix well with wooden spoon, now add this solution to 1 liter mug 
8. Always cover the Chlorine preparation.

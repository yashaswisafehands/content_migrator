# Chapter: Preamble

This is a suggestion of how to treat malaria.

HOWEVER, the patterns of resistance to antimalarial drugs vary across countries, making it difficult to produce a universal guide on malaria treatment.

Or follow your national guidelines.

# Indications and Usage

Treatment of uncomplicated malaria.

# Dosage and Administration

## Adults:

6 doses of artemether 20 mg/ lumefantrine 120 mg (4 tablets) orally at 0, 8, 24, 36, 48 and 60 hours after diagnosis.

# Contraindications

None. Caution should be taken at severe liver insufficiency.

# Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are gastro-intestinal reactions, skin itching and rash, headache and dizziness.

Allergic reactions are rare.

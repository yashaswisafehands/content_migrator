# Chapter: Legal Provision for safe abortion services in Ethipoia

According to Article 551 of the penal code of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, termination of pregnancy is permissible by a recognized medical institution in cases of:

- Rape or incest, 
- Risk to mother or child,
- Serious fetal deformity,
- Physical or mental deficiency,
- Immediate intervention in cases of grave and imminent danger that can be averted by immediate intervention, in accordance with the provision of Article- 75 of this code.

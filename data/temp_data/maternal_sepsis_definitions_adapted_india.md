### Diagnosis of Sepsis

A woman is considered to have sepsis when there is a suspected or confirmed infection along with signs of mild to moderate organ dysfunction.

The signs of infection include:

- Temperature more than 38°C or 100.4°F 
- Abnormal or foul-smelling vaginal discharge 
- Lower abdominal pain 
- Tender uterus

Signs of mild to moderate Organ dysfunction includes:

- Hypotension- Systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg,
- Reduced urine output below 30 ml per hour.
- Tachycardia - Pulse 110 beats per minute or more
- Tachypnoea – respiratory rate more than 22 breaths per minute
- Altered mental status such as drowsiness or confusion

Other symptoms of shock include: Pale skin, sweatiness or cold clammy skin, rapid pulse, difficulty breathing, dizziness and fainting.

### Prerequisites for vacuum extraction

The prerequisites for vacuum extraction in second stage of labour are:

- Cephalic presentation, where the foetal head is no more than 1/5 palpable above pubic bone.

![Image](bed8c2d1072b48f2adb24430db949c10)

- Position occiput posterior.

![Image](d59240e866dd4eaf85740f1d0eab0956)

- Position occiput anterior.

![Image](26015827356f41e2b76b6d51f80303f7)

- Cervix fully dilated.

- Membranes ruptured.

- Bladder empty.

- Gestational age of at least 36 weeks.

- Relevant expertise.

- You must be willing to stop.

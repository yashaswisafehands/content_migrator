# Chapter: Chest compressions and ventilation

## When to initiate chest compression:

- **Heart rate < 60 bpm** after 30 seconds of effective ventilation. 
- Begin Chest Compressions with Bag and Mask Ventilation (PPV).

**Compression to Ventilation Ratio:**

- For every 3 compressions, deliver 1 breath. 
- 90 Chest Compressions: 30 Breaths per minute.

![Image](17811a96f2a64fc18ddffebf97ef45f6)

## Techniques for Chest Compressions:

**1.** **Thumb Technique:**

- Support the spine and place thumbs just below the line connecting the nipples on the sternum.
- Compress the chest to about one third of its antero-posterior diameter.

2. Finger Technique:

- Place the newborn on flat and firm surface.
- Use the middle and index or ring fingers to compress the sternum. 
- Ensure fingers stay in contact with the chest on release to avoid shifting the compression area.

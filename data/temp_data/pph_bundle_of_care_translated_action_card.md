# Chapter: PPH bundle of care 

# Postpartum Haemorrhage Treatment Care Bundle

The PPH care bundle should be initiated within 15 minutes after diagnosing a postpartum haemorrhage.

The PPH care bundle includes the following steps done rapidly and simultaneously:

- **Uterus massage**

Massage until the uterus has contracted, or when there is persistent bleeding.

- **Administer uterotonic agent and tranexamic acid**

Give **oxytocin** 10 IU IM/IV + **oxytocin drip**AND **tranexamic acid** 1g slowly IV. 

Misoprostol and ergometrine can also be used to treat PPH. See the drug list for further information.

- **Provide IV fluids**

Give 1-2 L of Normal Saline or Ringer's lactate.

- **Examination**

Ensure bladder is empty, manually evacuate clots, check the perineum and vagina internally for tears and inspect the placenta for completeness.

- **Escalation**

Escalate care if bleeding does not stop after taking these initial steps or you are unable to identify or manage the cause of bleeding.

Any available interventions from the PPH care bundle should be initiated immediately, even if the complete bundle cannot be completed.

If the woman has received all bundle interventions within the PPH treatment bundle but continues to bleed – call a senior clinical provider capable of providing further management.

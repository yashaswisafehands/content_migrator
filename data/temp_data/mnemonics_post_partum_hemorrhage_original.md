### Shake and shout

- Assess level of consciousness

### Call a CAB

> Call for help!

- **C**irculation – take pulse and blood pressure
- **A**irway – ensure it is open
- **B**reathing – take respiratory rate

### Immediate stabilization

- Stop the bleeding
- Support circulation
- Support airway
- Support breathing

### Secondary survey

## Big 5

1. CNS – AVPU
2. CVS – BP & P every 15 minutes
3. Respiration – RR, put on sat, monitor
4. Liver and GIT – look for jaundice
5. Renal -  measure hourly urine output, take blood for urea and creatinine

## Forgotten 4

1. Haematological system– take blood for haemoglobin, platelets, clotting profile
2. Endocrine system – measure blood glucose
3. Immunological system– HIV-status + TP
4. Musculoskeletal system – look for bleeding at drip sites indicating DIC

## Core 1

- Examine abdomen to check uterine contraction
- Examine placenta to see if it is complete
- Examine perineum and vagina for tears

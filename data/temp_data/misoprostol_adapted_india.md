# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Misoprostol is a synthetic prostaglandin E1 analogue used to stimulate uterine contractions during the third stage of labour and to manage postpartum haemorrhage.

**To prevent postpartum haemorrhage.**

**To treat postpartum haemorrhage:**It is indicated when intravenous oxytocin and tranexamic acid are unavailable or when bleeding does not respond to initial treatment.

# Dosage and Administration

For preventing post partum haemorrhage:

600 mcg (3 tablets) orally.

For treatment of post partum haemorrhage:

800 micrograms (4 tablets) sublingually.

Repeat 200-800 mcg.

**Maximum dose:** 1600 mcg.

# Contraindications

Prostaglandin allergy.

Prostaglandins should not be given to pregnant women as they cause abortion.

Prostaglandins should not be given to pregnant women as they cause abortion.

# Adverse reactions

Common side effects are shivering/chills, diarrhoea, abdominal pain, hyperthermia, nausea, vomiting, flatulence, constipation, dyspepsia, headache and menstrual irregularity.

Severe side effects include hypotension, sinus tachycardia, foetal bradycardia, uterine contractions, pain, vaginal bleeding

A few will experience a transient moderate increase in temperature.

Some will have nausea, vomiting, constipation or flatulence.

Less common: headache and dizziness is experienced.

Rarely Misoprostol can cause liver damage.

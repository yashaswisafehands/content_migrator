# Chapter: Overview of the different abortion methods

## ≤ 12-14 weeks:

**Medical abortion:**

- Avoids surgery 
- Mimics the process of miscarriage 
- Controlled by the woman
- May take place at home (<12 weeks)
- Takes hours/days to complete
- Timing may not be predictable 
- May require more clinical visits

![Image](b3d91efba13e48d893f833b9bed96eeb)

**Vacuum aspiration:**

- Quick procedure 
- Complete abortion easily verified by evaluation of aspired POC 
- Takes place at health care facility 
- Sterilization or placement of IUD
- Small risk of uterine/cervical injury
- Timing of abortion controlled by provider

![Image](5740716f93c345a8a5a6fb26ce1326ff)

## >12-14 weeks:

**Medical abortion:**

- Avoids surgery 
- Mimics the process of miscarriage
- Takes place In a health care facility 
- Takes hours/days to complete
- Timing may not me predictable
- Bleeding, cramps, nausea and vomiting
- Low risk of uterine rupture

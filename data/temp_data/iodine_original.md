# Chapter: Indications and Usage

- Prevention of cretinism

## Dosage and Administration:

It is recommended for pregnant women to take iodine-containing medications from the 12th week of pregnancy until childbirth.

- Iodine balance 200 mg (daily dose) orally
 
or 

- Potassium iodide 200 mg (daily dose) orally

## Contraindications and side effects:

- Hyperthyroidism
- Toxic thyroid adenoma
- Nodular goitre when used in doses of more than 300 µg/day
- Hypersensitivity to iodine

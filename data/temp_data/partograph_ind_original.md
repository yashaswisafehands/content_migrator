# Chapter: The partograph

Partograph is a graphic recording of the progress of labour and the condition of the mother and foetus.

It is a tool which helps assess the need for action and recognizes the need for referral at the appropriate time.

Start plotting the partograph when the woman enters in active phase of labour, i.e, cervical dilation ≥4 cm.

**Note- Each small boxes in the vertical column of the partograph represents a half-hour (30 minutes) interval.**

## The partograph has the following components:

![Image](4e20e0ebb3664dcc94d9d9ebee4d07a5)

1. Identification data
2. Foetal condition
3. Progress of labour
4. Maternal condition
5. Interventions

# Chapter: Foetal condition

On the foetal condition section, record foetal indicators such as:

### Foetal Heart Rate (FHR):

- Ensure to count FHR for one full minute immediately after contraction.
- Count FHR every half an hour and mark as a dot.
- Normal FHR is between 120–160 beats per minute.

![Image](4ee6b0cd6e464cbc843b3030329f78f2)

### Amniotic Fluid:

Check the status of membrane and colour of amniotic every half hourly using a sterile perineal pad and record as:

- **I** – Intact membranes
- **C** – Clear liquor
- **M** – Meconium-stained liquor
- **B**– Blood-stained liquor

# Chapter: Progress of labour

### Cervical dilatation:

- It is the first component to be plotted on the partograph.
- Plot initial finding at alert line, use an 'X' and note the time.
- Plot every 4 hours.

> Note- Plotting to the right of the Alert line indicates prolonged or obstructed labour.

![Image](1d51c2d6046b45a0a209277c64812fe8)

# Chapter: Maternal condition

This section is for information about maternal indicators such as:

- **Pulse:**Record maternal pulse every half hour and mark with a dot (.)
- **Blood pressure:** Plot every 4 hours using a vertical arrow with upper end signifying systolic BP and lower end diastolic BP
- **Temperature:** should be noted every 4 hours.

![Image](3f689366a48943ad8eaa57cb68d0f004)

# Chapter: Interpreting a partograph

- **Crossing the Alert line** (plotting to the right) indicates prolonged or obstructed labour.
- **Crossing of the Action line** (the plotting moves to the right of the Action line) indicates the need for intervention.
- If action line is crossed, the appropriate management should start or woman should reach FRU.
- FHR <120 beats/min or >160 beats/min indicates foetal distress.
- Inadequate uterine contractions mean no increase in duration, intensity, or frequency.
- Amniotic fluid abnormalities indicate presence of meconium or blood-stained liquor.
- When maternal vitals i.e. pulse, blood pressure, or temperature exceed normal limits (pulse >100/min, BP >140/90 mmHg, temp >38°C).

# Chapter: Indication and usage

Azithromycin is a broad-spectrum macrolide antibiotic used to treat a variety of bacterial infections.

Administered as prophylactic antibiotic prior to Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) or Medical methods of Abortion (MMA)

# Dosage and Administration

## Antibiotic prophylaxis for surgical abortion

Before the MVA procedure give 1 g oral a single dose is recommended.

Dose with Medical methods of abortion (MMA)- 500mg orally once dailya day for three days

# Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to any macrolide drugs

Previous jaundice or liver problems caused by taking azithromycin

# Adverse reactions

Common side effects include nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting, loss of appetite, headaches, feeling dizzy or tired.

Serious side effects are rare and include irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia), signs of liver or gallbladder problems (such as skin turning yellow or dark pee), tinnitus, severe stomach pain, diarrhoea that contains blood.

In rare cases it is possible to have a serious allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to azithromycin.

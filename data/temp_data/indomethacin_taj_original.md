# Chapter: Indications and use

Recommended for acute and maintenance tocolytic therapy in women with a high likelihood of preterm labour to improve the condition of the newborn, but only if:

- Spontaneous preterm labour is suspected or diagnosed.
- The gestational age is accurately known, between 24+0 and 33+6 weeks.
- There are no contraindications to the use of tocolytics such as vaginal haemorrhage, placental abruption or intrauterine infection.
- The use of tocolytics allows time for the use of ACTs and/or transfer of the mother to a higher-level facility.

# Dosage and administration

Indomethacin per rectum:

- Loading dose: 100 mg rectally;
- After 1 hour, maintenance dose: 25 mg every 6 hours for 48 hours
- Not used for more than 48 hours.

Indomethacin per os tocolysis technique:

- 50 mg indomethacin orally with Almagel;
- After 1 hour a maintenance dose of 25-50 mg orally every 4-6 hours for 48 hours; not to be used more than 48 hours.
- 4-6 hours for 48 hours

# Side effects:

- May lead early closure of the foetal ductus arteriosus (should not be should not be administered after 32 weeks of gestation
- Necrotising enterocolitis of the newborn)
- Intraventricular haemorrhage in neonates.

# Contraindications:

- Bronchial asthma
- Bleeding from the gastrointestinal tract, as well as
- A history of peptic ulcer disease
- Renal failure
- Coronary heart disease
- Anaemia
- Suspected fetal heart or kidney defects

**Note**:

- Should not be  used for more than 48 hours
- Women understand the benefits and risks of tocolytics, including the uncertainty of long-term health effects.

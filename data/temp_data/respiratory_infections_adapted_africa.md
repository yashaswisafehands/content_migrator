# Chapter: Symptoms

## Common symptoms of respiratory infections include:

- Fever ≥38°C  
- Tiredness
- Dry cough

**Other symptoms are:**

- Sore throat  
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath  
- Aches and pains  
- Diarrhea, nausea, or a runny nose

# Chapter: Minimize the spread at the health facility

## Managing placement

- Have a triage station to screen patients for respiratory infections at the facility entrance, before they can get to any waiting areas.  
- Immediately isolate suspected and confirmed cases of infection.
- Offer a mask to anyone suspected to be positive. 
- To reduce stress and anxiety, explain to patients what you do and why you do it. 
- If possible, place patients in private rooms.  
- Keep people with suspected and confirmed cases of infections separate.  
- Maintain at least a 1-metre distance between patients.

## Managing the environment

- Limit movement of the patient.  
- Perform regular cleaning.  
- Maintain good ventilation by opening windows and doors.

## Managing visitors

- Limit the number of visitors.  
- All visitors should wear the required Personal Protective Equipment.

# Chapter: Five steps to prevent respiratory infections

1. **Hands:** Perform Hand Hygiene frequently.  
2. **Elbow:** Cover your nose and mouth with a bent elbow or tissue when sneezing or coughing.  
3. **Face:** Avoid touching your eyes, nose and mouth with unclean hands.  
4. **Distance:** Stay at least a one metre distance from others - avoid physical contact (e.g., shaking hands, hugging, or kissing).  
5. **Feel:**If you feel unwell, stay home.

* Alcohol-based handrub is the fastest and most effective hand hygiene method and better tolerated by your skin than handwash.

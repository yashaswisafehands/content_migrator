### Emergency Management

With any signs of septic abortion

>

Uterine evacuation must be performed without delay.

Do not delay uterine evacuation to await “antibiotic coverage”!

> **CALL FOR HELP**

### Airway

Position to prevent airway occlusion or aspiration.

### Breathing

If gasping or not breathing, ventilate with bag and mask.

### Circulation

Insert 2 wide bore IV cannulas.

Administer normal saline or Ringer’s lactate **≥ 2 L IV within 1 hour.**

Insert an indwelling urinary catheter to monitor fluid balance.

### Drugs

Administer intravenous Aantibiotics intravenous according to local guidelines, for example:

**Ampicillin 2 g every 6 hours**

AND

**Gentamicin 5mg/kg  IV every 24 hrs**

AND

**Metronidazole 500 mg every 8 hrs**

If in a malaria risk area, 
treat according to local guidelines.

**Chloramphenicol** should be avoided in pregnant and lactating women.

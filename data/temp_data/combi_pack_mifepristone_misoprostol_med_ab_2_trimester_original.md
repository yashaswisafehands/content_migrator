# Chapter: Mifepristone & Misoprostol combination (2. trimester)

# Indication and usage

Mifepristone is a synthetic steroid that blocks progesterone, a hormone essential for maintaining early pregnancy. It is used for medical termination of pregnancy but must always be followed by misoprostol, not taken on its own.

Misoprostol, a prostaglandin E1 analogue, induces uterine contractions and may be used alone or together with mifepristone or letrozole for medical abortion or the treatment of incomplete abortion.

# Dosage and Administration

## Day 1: Mifepristone

Mifepristone 200 mg orally, followed by misoprostol regimen 1-2 days later.

## After 1-2 days: Misoprostol

Misoprostol 400 mg vaginally, buccally or sublingually every 3 hours.

The dose of misoprostol is reduced in the 2nd trimester due to the greater sensitivity of the uterus to prostaglandins.

# Contraindications for mifepristone & misoprostol combination

Previous allergic reaction to mifepristone or misoprostol

Known or suspected ectopic pregnancy

Inherited porphyria

Chronic adrenal failure

# Precautions

IUD in place (remove before treatment begins)

Serious/unstable health problems, including but not limited to haemorrhagic disorders, heart disease and severe anaemia

Severe uncontrolled asthma or long-term corticosteroid therapy

# Adverse reactions

Nausea, weakness, fever/chills, vomiting, headache, diarrhoea and dizziness.

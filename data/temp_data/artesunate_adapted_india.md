# Indications and Usage

Treatment of malaria.

First-line drug for severe malaria in all trimesters during pregnancy. Can be used for all types of malaria.

# Dosage and Administration

2.4 mg/kilo body weight IV (alternatively IM), repeated at 0, 12 and 24 hours, THEN once daily, until oral Artesunate 2 mg/kg can be taken, to complete a total course of 7 days.
Treatment should not exceed 7 days.

Thereafter, repeat the dose every 24 hours until oral therapy can be started:

Artesunate 2 mg/kg orally once daily until the 7-day regimen is complete.

Treatment should not exceed 7 days.

# Contraindications

Not specifically contraindicated, but caution in first trimester of pregnancy, can be used if benefits outweigh risks.

Caution should be taken at severe liver insufficiency.

# Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are Gastro-intestinal reactions, skin itching and rash, headache and dizziness.

Haemolytic anaemia with the need for blood transfusion is seen in up to 20% of clients treated with intravenous artesunate up to 30 days after the treatment.

Therefore it is recommended to check haemoglobin levels weekly up to 4 weeks after treatment.

Allergic reactions are rare.

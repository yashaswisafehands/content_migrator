# Indications and Usage

Nicardipine is used for severe hypertension and hypertensive emergencies.

## Severe hypertension:

Systolic BP ≥159 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥ 109 mmHg.

# Dosage and Administration

IV drip 5 mg/hour, titrated to 2.5 mg/hour every 5 minutes up to a maximum 10 mg/hour[FB14.1][PK14.2]

Injectable nicardipine is administered through an IV drip using an automatic pump with adjustable speed.

- • Monitor BP and heart rate every 5 minutes at the start of administration until the woman is stable. 
- • Continue to monitor the woman’s condition after IV nicardipine is completed.

# Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the drug

# Precaution

This drug should be used during pregnancy only if the benefit outweighs the risk of the foetus.

- • Interaction with other medicines 
- • Presence of other medical problems 
- • According to some authorities, use is contraindicated.  
- • Animal studies have shown an embryonical effect and foetal harm.  
- • In humans, transient foetal heart rate compromise and neonatal hypotension and asphyxia have been reported. 
- • Excreted into breast milk in small amounts. No adverse effects have been reported in exposed infants, but data are limited. Breastfeeding may be continued with routine clinical observation of the newborns’s general condition.

# Adverse Reactions

Caution in liver impairment, extensively metabolised by liver.
Always consult your healthcare professional to ensure the information displayed applies to your personal circumstances.

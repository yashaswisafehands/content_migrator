# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Prevention of pre-eclampsia

In group of risk:history of pre-eclampsia, diabetes, autoimmune diseases, multiple pregnancy, chronic hypertension.

# Dosage and administration

Aspirin 100 mg from 12 weeks to 34–35 weeks of pregnancy.

## Contraindications and side effects

- During the third trimester, it increases the risk of premature closure of the ductus arteriosus in the fetal heart.
- Gastrointestinal bleeding in the history, exacerbation of peptic ulcer disease, presence of bleeding from the gastrointestinal (or urogenital) tract, and severe liver dysfunction.

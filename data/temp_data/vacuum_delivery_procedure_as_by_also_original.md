### Procedure for vacuum extraction

## Go through A-J

**A**sk for help.

**A**ddress the patient, inform her and her companion what you are about to do while you continue to prepare, tell her that you need her to cooperate and keep pushing when there is a contraction.

**A**bdominal<color style="#b5093f"> </color>palpation – no more than 1/5 of the head should be palpable above the pubic bone.

**B**ladder<color style="#b5093f"> </color>empty.

**C**heck that **C**ervix is fully dilated.

**C**ontractions<color style="#b5093f"> </color>– there must be contractions to succeed.

**D**etermine position – locate the posterior fontanel (triangular shaped) with your index finger to enable correct positioning of the vacuum extractor.

**E**quipment<color style="#b5093f"> </color>ready – delivery tray, oxytocin 10 IU for IM injection (for active management of the third stage), towels, neonatal resuscitator, and vacuum extractor.

**F**lexion Point – most cups have a radius of 3 cm from their centre to their edge. Place the edge of the cup at the tip of the posterior triangular fontanel.

**F**eel for vaginal tissue between the cup and the foetal head before and after applying suction to avoid trauma to the vagina. Apply suction with adequate pressure to firmly attach the cup to the fetal scalp.

Gently, steady traction with no rocking during each contraction pulling downwards with the first contractions, then more upwards as the head is delivering.

**H**alt in between contractions.

**H**ALT and abandon after 3 pop-offs, or after 3 pulls with no progress, or after 30 minuites from application if delivery is not achieved.

Intact<color style="#b5093f"> </color>perineum!

When the head will be delivered during the next contraction, protect and support the perineum with one hand; squeeze with your index and thumb from either side of the perineum as the head is crowning to prevent overstretching, and lift the head with your third finger upward and away from the perineum, while the other hand keeps the head from coming too fast.

An **I**ncision is only rarely needed.

**J**aw<color style="#b5093f"> </color>reachable – release the vacuum and remove the cup.

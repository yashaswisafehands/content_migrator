## Indication and Usage

Deworming for all pregnant women in Soil-transmitted helminth (STH)endemic areas (prevalence more than 20%).

Administer once, for all pregnant women after the 1st trimester.

## Dosage and Administration

Single dose of 400 mg preferably within 2nd trimester.

## Contraindications

First trimester of pregnancy

## Adverse Reactions

Potential side effects like nausea, vomiting, rash, and abdominal pain, urticaria occur in some cases.

# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Cefazolin is active against many gram-positive aerobic cocci but has limited activity against gram-negative bacteria. It is poorly absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract and must be administered parenterally.

# Dosage and Administration

## Adults:

**Prophylaxis:** cefazolin 1-2 g IV once.

**Manual Removal of Placenta:** cefazolin 1 g IV (single dose).

**Third/fourth-degree tear:** cefazolin 2 g IV (AND  metronidazole 500 mg IV).

Maintenance dose (if recommended): cefazolin 1-2 g IV/ or IM every 8 hours for 5-10 days.

Once clinical improvement occurs, cefazolin may be substituted for cefalexin 500 mg orally every 6 hours.

# Contraindications

Allergy to β-lactamase antibiotics.

# Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are hypersensitivity and skin rashes.

Uncommon reactions are urticaria, bronchospasm, anaphylaxis, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and reversible cholestatic jaundice.

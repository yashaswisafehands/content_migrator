# Chapter: Intracervical block

Prepare an anesthetic: 5-10 ml of a 1% solution of Lidocaine

Inject half of the 1% Lidocaine dosage each at 4 o'clock and 8 o'clock position after withdrawing to ensure that the needle is not in the blood vessel.

Inject directly under the epithelium.

Do not go deeper than 3 mm.

Wait for 2 minutes for full effect.

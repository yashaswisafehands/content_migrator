### Emergency Management

A woman with sepsis needs urgent treatment. Manage the condition as  following:

>

Uterine evacuation must be performed without delay.

Do not delay uterine evacuation to await “antibiotic coverage”!

> **Call for help!**

### Airway

Position so airway is not occluded and suction the woman who is at risk of aspiration.

### Breathing

Give oxygen at rate of 6-8 litters per minute. If gasping or absent breathing, ventilate with bag and mask.

### Circulation

Insert IV cannula of size 16-18 gauge.

Give **Normal Saline**or **Ringer’s lactate** over the next 12 hours to maintain BP above 90 mm Hg and urine output above 50 ml per hour.

Insert indwelling urine catheter to monitor input and output.

### Drugs

If woman is very sick and cannot take oral medication, give following antibiotics:

<color style="blue">**Ampicillin**1g IV, 6 hourly</color>

AND

<color style="blue">**Gentamicin**80 mg IM,12 hourly</color>

AND

<color style="blue">**Metronidazole**500 mg IV, 8 hourly</color>

If in a malaria risk area, routine testing is to be done   for treatment, give Quinine 10 mg thrice a day.

**Primaquine** should be avoided in pregnant  women.

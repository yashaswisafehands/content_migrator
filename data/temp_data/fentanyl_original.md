# Chapter: Indication and Usage

Strong opioid analgetic drug

# Dosage and administration

## Adults:

50-100 mcg IV or 50-100mcg IM

IV: Give immediately before procedure

IM: Give 30-60 minutes before procedure

Can be repeated if insufficient response after 10-15 minutes up to a maximum dose of 250mcg over 24 hours

# Contraindications

If signs of respiratory depression (respiratory rate below 12 times per minutes), another dose should not be provided.

If difficulty with breathing, support by respiratory treatment and use antidote agent Naloxone IV or IM 0.4mg every 2 hours until antidote effect appears.

# Adverse reactions

Adverse reactions may include Dizziness, headache, exhaustion, decreased heart rate, low respiratory rate, unconsciousness, low blood pressure, convulsion.

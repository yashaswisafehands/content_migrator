# Chapter: Mild (Flu-like symptoms, anosmia, Myalgia)

## 1st and 2nd trimester

- Home isolation
- Symptomatic management (Antipyretics, hydration)
- In consultation with obstetrician and physician
- Review visit after 2 weeks
- Sonographic assessment of fetal anatomy/growth as appropriate

## 3rd trimester

- Beyond period of viability
- Home isolation in majority
- Decision for delivery to be individualized based on obstetric indication and need

---

# Chapter: Moderate (Dyspnea, Refractory fever, Pneumonia, Abnormal Blood Gas)

- Admit in ward 
- Investigate
- CBC, KFT, CRP, ESR
- Procalcitonin, IL6, LDH, Ferritin& D-dimer every 48-72 hours

## Danger signs (progression of illness)

- SOB 
- Tachpnea 
- sPO2<94% 
- Persistent fever beyond 5-7 days 
- Inability to tolerate oral fluids 
- Confusion / lethargy

## 1st and 2nd trimester

- Steroids 
- Anticoagulation
- Antibiotics (if needed)

## 3rd trimester

Beyond period of viability decision for delivery to be based on obstetric indication or worsening maternal condition.

---

# Chapter: Severe

RR>30 min

(SpO2<94%)(SMFM)

- Admit in HDU/ICU
- Investigate
- CBC, KFT, CRP, ESR
- Procalcitonin, IL6, LDH, Ferritin& D-dimer every 48-72 hours

- O2 support / NIV / Mechanical Ventilation
- Steroids
- Anticoagulation
- Antibiotics (for community acquired pneumonia)
- Decision for delivery to be considered for refractory hypoxemia > 32 weeks

# Chapter: Antenatal Corticosteroid Therapy

Antenatal corticosteroid therapy (ACT) is recommended for women with a high likelihood of preterm birth from 24 weeks to 34 weeks of gestation.

ACT is given to women with true preterm labour, preterm premature membrane rupture, severe pre-eclampsia or planned preterm birth by induction or caesarean section.

ACT helps to reduce neonatal death, respiratory distress syndrome and systemic infections in preterm newborns by maturing their lungs. ACT has maximal effect if the foetus is delivered 24 hours after the last dose and up to 7 days after.

Start ACT, even if you suspect that there is not enough time to complete the course before delivery.

> Frank Chorioamnionitis is an absolute contraindication for ACT. Ask for history of fever, lower abdominal pain; look for foul smelling vaginal discharge, tachycardia, uterine tenderness and foetal tachycardia, which are indicative of chorioamnionitis.

## Medication and Dosage

4 doses of 6 mg **dexamethasone** (IM) given 12 hours apart

**OR**

2 doses of 12 mg **betamethasone** (IM) given 24 hours apart

# Chapter: Cleaning with Alcohol, Chlorine, and Hypochlorite

### Cleaning with Alcohol, Chlorine, and Hypochlorite

As per NMC guideline: 70% ethyl alcohol can be used to disinfect small areas between use, such as reusable dedicated equipment (for example, thermometers).

All surfaces including beds, tabletops should be cleaned with 0.5% chlorine solution or 0.5% sodium hypochlorite solution at least every 6-8 hours in noncritical areas and every 3-4 hours in critical areas.

Hypochlorite is rapidly inactivated in the presence of organic material; therefore, regardless of the concentration used, it is important to first clean surfaces thoroughly with soap and water or detergent using mechanical action such as scrubbing or friction.

> Caution: High concentrations of chlorine can lead to corrosion of metal (reason why it is no longer recommended to clean utensils in chlorine) and irritation of skin or mucous membrane, in addition to potential side-effects related to chlorine smell for vulnerable people such as people with asthma.

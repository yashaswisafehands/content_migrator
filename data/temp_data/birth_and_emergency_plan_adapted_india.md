# Chapter: Birth and emergency plan

## Birth preparedness and complication readiness (BPCR)

Develop the birth and emergency plan by interacting with the woman and her family.

![Image](95d12f6b67a94e42b3ff271a786fa35f)

- Explain the importance of delivery in a facility and identify the place of birth.
- Teach signs of onset of labour and danger signs. Instruct to contact health facility immediately, if they occur.
- Identify birth companion and accompanying person.

![Image](969375c36bec417aace974515f9dfb55)

- Discuss on transportation arrangements, to call102 & 108 services.
- Support in calculating an estimate of expenses and advise to keep an emergency fund in case of complications.
- A blood donor should be identified for any unforeseen emergencies.
- Advise to have the maternal record and clean clothes for mother and newborn with the necessary sanitary supplies ready to carry to the health facility.

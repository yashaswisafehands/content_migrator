### APGAR Score

Assess the newborn with APGAR score 1, 5 and 10 minutes after birth.

**APGAR Score 7-10: Healthy**

> APGAR Score 0-6: Require medical attention

[TableStart]
|                         | 0 points           | 1 point                       | 2 points      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------- |
| Activity                | Limb arms and legs | Arms and legs flexed          | Active        |
| Pulse                   | Absent             | Less than 100                 | Above 100     |
| Grimace                 | No response        | Grimace                       | Sneeze, cough |
| Appearance (skin color) | Blue or pale       | Body pink, arms and legs blue | All pink      |
| Respiration             | Absent             | Slow, irregular               | Crying        |
| Total score             |                    |                               |               |
[TableEnd]

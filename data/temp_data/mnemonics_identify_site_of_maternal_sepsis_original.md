### Big 5

1. CNS: Meningitis
2. CVS: Bacterial endocarditis
3. Respiratory system: Pneumonia, TB
4. Liver and GIT: Hepatitis, Cholangitis, Pancreatitis, Appendicitis, Typhoid
5. Renal system: Pyelonephritis

### Forgotten 4

1. Haematological system: Malaria
2. Endocrine system: Mastitis
3. Immunological system: HIV
4. Musculoskeletal system – cellulitis, DVT

### Core 1

- Endometritis, chorioamnionitis

The most common error in the management of women with sepsis that subsequently die, is underestimating the severity of the disease by not assessing each organ system appropriately.

## Indications and Usage

- Reduces the risk of pre-eclampsia and pre-term birth.

Given to pregnant women during pregnancy and lactation.

## Dosage and Administration

One tablet containing 500 mg elemental calcium and 250 IU vitamin D3 twice a day.  

Start from 14 weeks and continue until 6 months after delivery. 

Take preferably between meals .

Advice a gap of 2 hours between iron and calcium supplements for better absorption of both.

## Contraindications

None if taken within the recommended limit (1g/day).

## Adverse reactions

A small proportion of women may experience mild gastritis so calcium tablets should be taken with meals.

Excessive consumption (>3 gm/d) may increase the risk of urinary stones and Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) and reduce the absorption of essential micronutrients.

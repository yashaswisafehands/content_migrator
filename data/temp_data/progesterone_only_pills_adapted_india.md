## Introduction

Progestin-only pills also called “Minipills” contain very low doses of a synthetic hormone. It inhibits = the ovulation, thickening of cervical mucus and thinning  endometrial lining.

## Medications

- **Levonorgestrel** (LNG)
- **Desogestrel**

## Common side-effects

Some report experiencing: changes to bleeding patterns, acne, breast tenderness, changes in sex drive, mood changes, headaches, dizziness, nausea and vomiting.

Postpartum Amenorrhea may be prolonged in breastfeeding woman.

## Contraindications

Women with history of breast cancer, acute blood clot in deep veins of legs, severe liver disease, infection or tumour and those who take medicines for seizures.

# Chapter: Alert values for the first and second stages

The alert values for the first and second stages of active labour according to the Labour Care Guide.

## Alert values 1st stage

- **5 cm = ≥6 h:** the cervical dilation remains at 5 cm for 6 hours or more.
- **6 cm = ≥5 h**: the cervical dilation remains at 6 cm for 5 hours or more. 
- **8 cm = ≥2.5 h:** the cervical dilation remains at 8 cm for 2.5 hours or more.
- **9 cm = ≥2h:** the cervical dilation remains at 9 cm for 2 hours or more.

## Alert values 2nd stage

A prolonged second stage (from full cervical dilation to birth) is diagnosed **after 3 hours**in nulliparous women, and **2 hours** in multiparparous women.

An alert is triggered if there is no progress and if the time for the first or second stage exceeds the stated values.

# Chapter: Neonatal Referral

## Refer newborn to a to a higher centre if;

- Newborns require PPV for more than one minute, OR
- need for further resuscitation with chest compressions and/or drugs

## Assess and stabilize newborn prior to transport

![Image](18c1d6ac6eeb485ca95404d5fb9441c9)

- Do not refer the newborn if emergency is not controlled.
- Refer the newborn in a stable condition.
- Give or arrange medications as per doctor’s advice.

## Communication:

- Communicate with the family and mother regarding condition and reason for referral.
- Communicate with referral facility regarding condition of newborn, approximately time of arrival, working diagnosis and what has already been done. Explain the condition of the newborn to the accompanying family members and give specific instructions to be followed enroute.

## Pre-referral and during transport stabilization:

- Newborn need to be stabilized for four vital parameters (TOPS) before and during transport:

**A. Temperature:**

- Keep the newborn warm, before and during referral (Maintain temperature 36.5-37.5°C).
- Wrap the newborns well in layers with head, hands and feet covered
- Provide skin to skin contact or KMC during transport.
- Windows of transport vehicle to be kept closed to prevent heat loss.
- If available, transport newborn using a transport incubator.

**B. Airway & Oxygenation:**

- Ensure clear airway and breathing.
- Maintain saturation between 91-95%.
- Newborns who need oxygen should be transported with nasal cannula or prongs attached to an oxygen source with flow of 0.5-1 L/min.

**C. Perfusion** (adequate perfusion is indicated if):

- **HR**: 120-160 bpm and 
- **CRT** < 3 sec.

**D. Blood Sugar:**

- Maintained blood glucose >45 mg/dl. 
- Check blood sugar before referral and after arrival at the health facility.

## Referral note

The referral note should mention the following:

- Case particulars (name, age, gender, address)
- Chief complaints
- Condition on arrival at health facility (TOPS)
- Management and treatment details provided for stabilization, including antibiotics
- Reason for referral
- Condition at referral
- Contact details of the referring health personnel
- All available reports

**Arrange for a health care provider to accompany, where feasible**

## Arrange vehicle for transport

- Arrange the hospital vehicle or a vehicle available under the JSSK.
- The transport vehicle should be equipped to shift the newborn in a secure manner and stabilize the newborn en-route.

## Essential emergency drugs:

- Phenobarbital for the newborn in case of convulsions
- Adrenaline for the newborn in case of cardiac arrest

## Supplies:

- Appropriate clothing for newborn (cotton sheets/cap, woollen blanket)
- Thermometer
- Cotton swabs
- Feeding tubes
- Mucus sucker/suction catheter
- Oxygen source and nasal prongs
- Newborn AMBU bag with appropriate mask size (size 0 for preterm and size 1 for term newborn)
- Pulse oximeter
- Syringes of different size
- Examination and sterile gloves

## Monitoring during transport:

- If the newborn not breathing well or heart rate is less than 100b/min, continue ventilation while referring.

Continue monitoring and maintaining **TOPS** during transportation:

- **T**emperature
- **O**xygen saturation
- **P**eripheral circulation 
- **S**ugar (feeding

**NOTE:**
If a SNCU or referral unit is very far or not available, the newborn should be managed in the NBSU.

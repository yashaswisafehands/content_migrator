# Chapter: Five key moments for hand hygiene

The following are five key moments for performing hand hygiene:

1. **Before touching the woman or the newborn**(before taking vital signs and listening to the foetal heart rate). 
2. **Before a clean/aseptic procedure**(before vaginal examination, cutting and clamping the cord and taking blood tests). 
3. **After bodily fluid exposure risk** (after vaginal examination and delivering the placenta). 
4. **After touching the woman or the newborn** (after touching skin and performing bathing). 
5. **After touching the woman’s/newborn’s surroundings** (after touching the woman’s or newborn’s bed space  or the chart on the bedside).

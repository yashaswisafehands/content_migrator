# Chapter: Indication and Usage

Tetracycline is used to treat bacterial infections that are sensitive to the drug. Tetracycline crosses the placenta. If used during pregnancy, it can cause permanent staining of the teeth and affect bone growth in the infant. There is a risk that the mother will develop hepatotoxicity.

Tetracycline is excreted in breast milk in small amounts. Data is insufficient to determine whether the use is harmful to the child. Therefore, we do not recommend the use of tetracycline for pregnant and lactating women and recommend other antibiotics instead.

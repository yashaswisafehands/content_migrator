## Introduction

Injectable Contraceptive - Medroxy Progesterone (MPA) is an aqueous suspension of microcrystal for depo injection of pregnane 17 alfa - hydroxyprogesterone – derivative progestine medroxyprogesterone acetate.

It slowly releases progesterone and  inhibits ovulation,thickens cervical mucus, thinning  endometrial lining

MPA is a Progestogen-only Injectable (POI) given deep intra-muscular every three months.

## Medication examples

- **Medroxy Progesterone Acetate**(MPA)
- **Intramuscular -**one vial of 150 mg per 100 ml, aqueous suspension of MPA
- **Subcutaneous -**104 mg per 0.65 ml suspension of MPA

## Common side-effects

Changes in bleeding patterns, even in the months following cessation, weight changes, headaches, acne, hair loss, decreased sex drive and mood swings.

## Contraindications

Breastfeeding woman less than six weeks postpartum, BP ≥160/100 mmHg, unexplained vaginal bleeding, diabetes, ischaemic heart disease, stroke, acute DVT, liver tumours, and breast cancer.

# Chapter: Letrozole & Misoprostol (1. trimester)

# Indication and usage

Letrozole & misoprostol are two oral medicines that, when taken in the correct sequence, can be used for termination of pregnancy (induced medical abortion).

Letrozole is a third-generation selective aromatase inhibitor. Its mechanism of action involves suppression of oestrogen levels, which modifies the progesterone receptor concentration, subsequently leading to pregnancy loss.

Misoprostol, a prostaglandin E1 analogue, induces uterine contractions and may be used alone or together with mifepristone or letrozole for medical abortion or the treatment of incomplete abortion.

# Dosage and administration

## Day 1-3: Letrozole

Letrozole 10mg orally daily for 3 days, before misoprostol administration on day 4.

## Day 4: Misoprostol

Misoprostol 800mg sublingually, buccally or vaginally

Repeating the dose of misoprostol every 4 hours can be considered until successful completion of abortion is achieved.

Vaginal bleeding will usually start after taking misoprostol (occasionally after taking letrozole), accompanied by abdominal pain and cramping.

## Below 9 weeks

Women may take misoprostol at home if there are no complications. It is safe, usually more private, and more convenient.

# Contraindications for letrozole & misoprostol combination

Previous allergic reaction to letrozole or misoprostol

Known or suspected ectopic pregnancy

Inherited porphyria

Chronic adrenal failure

# Precautions

IUD in place (remove before treatment begins)

Serious/unstable health problems, including but not limited to haemorrhagic disorders, heart disease and severe anaemia.

Severe uncontrolled asthma or long-term corticosteroid therapy

# Adverse reactions:

Nausea, weakness, fever/chills, vomiting, headache, diarrhoea and dizziness

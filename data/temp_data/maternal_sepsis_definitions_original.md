### Diagnosis of Sepsis

To diagnose of sepsis in the presence of suspected infection, at least 2 out of the 3 following symptoms must be observed:

- Systolic blood pressure < 100 mmHg AND/OR
- Respiratory rate ≥ 22 AND/OR
- Any altered state of consciousness (for example drowsiness)

Septic shock is diagnosed when the following findings persist despite adequate fluid resuscitation

- Systolic blood pressure < 90 mmHg, OR;
- Urine output < 30 ml per hour.
- Pulse ≥ 110 pbm

Other symptoms of shock include: 
Pale skin, sweatiness  or cold clammy skin, rapid breathing, any altered state of consciousness (for example drowsiness)

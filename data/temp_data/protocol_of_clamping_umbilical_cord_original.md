# Chapter: Protocol of clamping umbilical cord

In cases where a newborn does not experience asphyxia and does not need immediate resuscitation:

- Conduct the clamping of the umbilical cord after the end of pulsation (but not later than the 3rd minute after the birth of the child);

- put a Kocher at a distance of 14 cm from the base of the umbilical ring;

- then apply a disposable plastic clip at a distance of 1.5 - 2 cm from the base of the umbilical ring;

•  cover 1,5 - 2 cm of the plastic clamp with a sterile cloth (to prevent splattering of blood) and cross the cord with sterile scissors.

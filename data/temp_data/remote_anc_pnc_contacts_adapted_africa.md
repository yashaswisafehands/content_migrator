# Chapter: Remote ANC contacts

## ANC schedule

Wherever possible, the current WHO recommendation of eight ANC contacts should be provided.

Where technology and services are available, some of these contacts may be remote (provided by phone, via WhatsApp, Skype or Facetime).

This may be appropriate following an individual risk assessment and can be used for appointments where the woman does not require physical clinical assessments or tests.

The schedule suggests which contacts could be undertaken face-to-face and which might suit a remote contact.

- **12 weeks:** Face-to-face   
- **20 weeks:** <color style="blue">Remote contact </color> 
- **26 weeks:**<color style="blue">Remote contact </color> 
- **30 weeks:** Face-to-face   
- **34 weeks:**<color style="blue">Remote contact</color>  
- **36 weeks:** Face-to-face  
- **38 weeks:** <color style="blue">Remote contact</color>  
- **40 weeks:** Face-to-face

# Chapter: Remote PNC contacts

## PNC schedule

Wherever possible, the current WHO recommendation of four PNC contacts should be provided.

Where technology and services are available, some of these contacts may be remote (provided by phone, via WhatsApp, Skype or Facetime).

This may be appropriate following an individual risk assessment and can be used for appointments where the woman/newborn does not require physical clinical assessments or tests.

The schedule below suggests which contacts could be undertaken face-to-face and which might suit a remote contact.

**Birth (24 hours):**Face-to-face

Initial face-to-face contact.  
Assess infection risk and wear recommended PPE.

**Day 3 (48-72 hours):** <color style="blue">Remote</color>

Includes ongoing risk assessment for the mother and the newborn.

**Week 2 (7-14 days):** Face-to-face

Face-to-face.  
Assess infection risk and wear recommended PPE.

**Week 6 (≈ 42 days):** <color style="blue">Remote</color>

Includes ongoing risk assessment of the mother and the newborn. 

Ensure contraceptive needs have been discussed and addressed.

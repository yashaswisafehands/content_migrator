### Emergency management for septic abortion

This is a BEmONC Signal Function. However, as a health care professional you still have to act according to the local policy and should only perform procedures that you have been authorized for. Septic abortion can be fatal within hours.

At signs of septic abortion or at excessive vaginal bleeding:

> Evacuation of the uterus must be performed with no delay!

Do not delay by awaiting "antibiotic coverage"!

> **CALL FOR HELP!**

### **Airway**

Position so airway is not occluded or at risk of aspiration.

### **Breathing**

Give oxygen at the rate of 6-8 L/min If gasping or breathing is absent ventilate with bag and mask.

### **Circulation**

Insert 2 wide bore IV cannulas.

Give resuscitation fluid at least <color style="blue">**1 liter IV, normal saline </color>or<color style="blue**"> Ringer’s lactate </color>fast in15-20 minutes.

Insert indwelling urine catheter to monitor in- and output.

### **Drugs**

Give prophylactic antibiotics intravenously and evacuate the uterus IMMEDIATELY!

<color style="blue">**Ampicillin**1 g/6 hrs.</color>

AND

<color style="blue">**Gentamicin** 80 mg/12 hrs.</color>

AND

<color style="blue">**Metronidazole** 500 mg/8 hrs.</color>

> This must not delay the lifesaving EVACUATION of the uterus.

This must not delay the lifesaving EVACUATION of the uterus.

Give antibiotics while you start the procedure.

If antibiotics are not available, perform evacuation, give antibiotics as soon as possible afterwards.

### **Evacuate Uterus**

Give Ibuprofen 400-800 milligrams orally for pain relief.

Upto 12 weeks of gestation Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) is the preferred method.

If the gestational age is beyond 12 weeks, evacuation is by Dilatation and Evacuation (D &E) and should be performed in a referral facility.

PERIPHERAL LEVEL

If at a peripheral level without possibility of performing manual vacuum aspiration: stabilize her, start Antibiotics and refer IMMEDIATELY.

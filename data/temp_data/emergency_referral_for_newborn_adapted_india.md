# Chapter: Emergency Referral

### Neonatal Transport

>

Newborns who are seriously ill and cannot be managed at the NBSU should be transferred to an SNCU.

Timely transport should be arranged, and appropriate care must be provided during the transfer.

If a newborn needs to be transferred to a higher-level facility: administer the recommended antibiotics prior to transfer.

Before and during transfer, it is important to keep the newborn warm. Use either skin-to-skin contact or wrap in a soft, dry cloth and blanket, ensuring that the head is covered.

Initiate breastfeeding or provide alternative breastmilk feeding.

Inform the referral centre.

Accompany the mother with the newborn.

It is crucial that the newborn is accompanied by a healthcare professional who is trained in managing an emergency.

Quickly organize transport and possible financial aid.

Consider bringing a relative who can donate blood.

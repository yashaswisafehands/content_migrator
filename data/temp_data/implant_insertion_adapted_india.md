# Chapter: Implant insertion

An implant can be inserted anytime of the month if you are reasonably certain the An implant can be inserted anytime of the month if you are reasonably certain the woman is not pregnant. If inserted within the first 7 days of the menstrual cycle, no back up contraception method is needed. If inserted after day 7, the woman should use back up contraception (e.g., condoms) for 7 days.

Implants can be inserted immediately after childbirth, irrespective of breastfeeding. It can also be inserted within 7 days After surgical abortion and on 3rd day after medical abortion protocol.

## Sterile equipment

- Sterile tray
- Ring / Sponge holding forceps
- Bowl with cotton balls 
- Sterile gauze 
- Sterile cut sheet
- Sterile Gloves 
- Sterile applicator with the preloaded Implant

## Other supplies

- Antiseptic solution 
- Syringe with 22 gauge needle 
- 1% Lignocaine
- Pressure Bandage
- Measuring scale
- Pen to mark the skin

## Insertion procedure

Greet the woman, confirm eligibility, obtain consent, explain the procedure, and address questions.

1. Check if insertion arm is clean, if not, ask woman to wash with soap and water and dry it.
2. Help the woman to lie on her back with arm flexed at elbow and hand behind her head.
3. Mark the skin on the inner side of the woman’s non-dominant upper arm by measuring 8-10 cm from the medial epicondyle of humerus. Ensure the insertion site is 3 to 5 cm below the sulcus, over the triceps. From insertion point, mark a straight line of 5 cm towards the shoulder. 
4. Wash hands and put on sterile gloves. Place equipment on a sterile field and clean the medial aspect of the arm twice with antiseptic and wait for 2minutes. 
5. Inject 1-2 ml of 1% Lignocaine under her skin at the intended insertion site. Do not go too deep.
6. Wait for 2-3 minutes before checking anaesthetic effectiveness. with point of needle.
7. Hold the implant applicator at textured surface. Remove needle shield by sliding it and visually check presence of implant inside needle.
8. Stretch the skin around insertion site  with the free hand.
9. Puncture the skin with the tip of the needlethe skin at 30o a shallow angle with the applicator and insert only up to the bevel. lift and straighten it to ‘tent’ the skin while gently pushing the device under the skin towards the second mark.
10. Do not insert the implant too deep. Lower the applicator parallel to the skin and advance the needle by keeping it subdermal. If you feel any resistance, stop and try a different direction. 
11. After the complete insertion, hold the device still, insert the rod(s) by unlocking the slider by pushing it down.
12. Once the rod(s) is in place, gently remove the applicator in horizontal position. Verify the presence of implant by feeling both ends
13. Cover the puncture site with sterile gauze, check for bleeding. and wrap with a pressure bandage. 
14. Observe the woman for 15-20 minutes and give implant card. Advise the woman to keep the incision area dry for 5 days. Remove the inner dressing after 5 days.

## Documentation

Documentation should be complete and correct. Give a  implant card to the client after explaining it with the following information:

- Type of implant and date of insertion
- Site of implant insertion
- Date when the implant will need to be removed or replaced
- Post insertion instructions
- What to do if they have problems with or questions about their implant

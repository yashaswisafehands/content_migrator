# Chapter: IUCD removal

Never refuse or delay IUCD removal when a woman requests it. Removing IUCD can be done any time in her menstrual cycle. The procedure is the same for all IUCD types.

The woman’s fertility will return with no delay following removal.

## Equipment and supplies:

- Sterile tray
- Sims or Cuscos Speculum
- Ring or sponge holding forceps
- Vulsellum
- Straight artery forceps
- Sterile gloves
- Bowl with cotton balls 
- Antiseptic solution
- Light source

## Procedure

1. Ensure that the woman is counselled, procedure explained and informed consent is obtained. 
2. Inform the woman that she may experience cramps for a short while as it comes out.
3. Woman must empty her bladder, wash perineal area and  lie flat on their back, in a private and clean environment.
4. Insert speculum gently into the vagina to visualise the cervix and IUCD strings.**(For missing strings, first rule out pregnancy, gently probe the cervical canal with a sterile long artery forceps to locate and draw out strings to remove IUCD. If strings cannot be found, refer to a specialist and consider ultrasound or X-ray to check IUCD position, malposition, or expulsion.)**
5. Carefully clean the cervix and vagina with antiseptic.
6. Ask the woman to take slow, deep breaths and relax.
7. Hold the anterior lip of the cervix with a vulsellum.
8. Grasp the strings with straight artery forceps at the cervical os
9. Gently but steadily pull the strings until the IUCD comes completely out of the cervix.
10. Show removed IUCD to the woman and insert another IUCD immediately following removal if desired by the woman.
11. Gently remove the vulsellum and check for any bleeding.
12. Maintain records and fill the IUCD card.

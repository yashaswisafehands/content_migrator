# Chapter: Usage

### Foetal heart rate - usage

![Image](80223ebfaa1b45ef922eb264852f9c7b)

Measurement of the foetal heart rate in childbirth is indicated:

**1)**On admission,

**2)** During the 1st stage of delivery: Monitor every 1 hour in the latent phase and every 30 minutes in the active phase.

**3)** Immediately after every contraction and at least every five minutes in the 2nd stage of delivery.

# Chapter: Normal Values

### Foetal heart rate - normal values

The lower limit for a normal foetal heart rate is defined as 120 beats per minute (bpm).

A heart rate below 120 bpm indicates foetal distress.

The upper limit for a normal heart rate is 160 bpm.

A heart rate above 160 bpm indicates foetal distress.

# Chapter: Practical information

### Foetal heart rate - practical information

The foetal heart is best heard by placing the foetoscope over the back of the baby.

![Image](0b0ac97d3c214cc8936abff749f0cc36)

Always measure the foetal heart rate for one minute immediately after a contraction (you will need a watch).

Measuring the maternal pulse and the foetal heart rate simultaneously can exclude a mix-up between the two.

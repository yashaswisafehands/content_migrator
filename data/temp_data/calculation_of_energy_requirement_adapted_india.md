# Chapter: Calculation of energy requirement

## Calculation of energy requirement

- Energy requirement includes normal adult requirement, additional requirement for foetal growth and increase in body weight of pregnant women.
- It does not increase in the first trimester unless a woman is underweight but increases during the second and third trimester.
- Energy requirement is calculated as per the pre-pregnancy weight and BMI of the woman.
- Severe caloric restriction or hypocaloric diet is not recommended for women with GDM. 
- An addition of 350 kcal/day is recommended for pregnant women.

# Chapter: Glasgow Coma Scale

### Glasgow Coma Scale

Give points according to the patient’s level of consciousness in the three categories listed below.

15 points are normal.

Below 15 points means any kind of affected cerebral condition.

### Eye opening response

4 points: Spontaneously

3 points: To speak

2 points: To pain

1 point: No response

![Image](1d48d41693044f559daf3d46d508e4cb)

### Verbal response

5 points: Oriented to time, person, and place

4 points: Confused

3 points: Inappropriate words

2 points: Incomprehensible sounds

1 point: No response

![Image](bb154c00bb624b0d81789edee0c1098f)

### Motor response

6 points: Obeys commands

5 points: Moves to localized pain

4 points: Flex to withdraw from pain

3 points: Abnormal flexion

2 points: Abnormal extension

1 point: No response

![Image](4c72c89060984a63845aa0e5b101bcb5)

If the total amount of points is below 15 it counts as one of the two mandatory parameters in the diagnosis of a sepsis.

Follow the condition by repeating the tests regularly and note date, clock, blood pressure, pulse, and respiratory rate.

# Chapter: Indications and usage

Sulfamethoxazole is indicated in combination with trimethoprim, for the following infections caused by bacteria:

Wound infections, urinary tract infections, acute otitis media in pediatric patients (when clinically indicated), acute exacerbations of chronic bronchitis in adults.

# Dosage and administration

400mg 2 tablets orally 2 times daily, for 5 days, combination with Trimethoprim 80mg

# Contraindications

Relative contraindications include folate deficiency, liver dysfunction, and renal insufficiency.

Generally use of Sulfamethoxazole with Trimethoprim should be avoided during the 1st trimester (because neural tube defects are a risk) and near term.

However, if use of this drug cannot be avoided during the 1st trimester, folic acid supplementation (4 mg/day) is necessary.

# Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are nausea, vomiting and rash.

If it used prolongedly, folate deficiency (resulting in macrocytic anemia) can also occur.

### Identify the cause

**Assess the 6 W’s:**

### Womb

If chorioamnionitis is diagnosed, delivery should typically occur within 12 hours.

If endometritis (puerperal sepsis) is diagnosed, evacuate the uterus if there is no improvement after 24 hours.

### Weaning

Mastitis or abscess:

Treat with **dicloxacillin**and empty the breasts.

Support the breasts using a bra or a binder.

Apply cold compresses between feeds to reduce swelling and pain.

Provide pain relief:****
Ibuprofen 200-400 mg AND/OR
Paracetamol 500-1000 mg****

### Wind

Pneumonia

Treat with **penicillin**.

### Water

Urinary tract infection

Treat with **ampicillin**and **gentamicin**.

### Wound

Add **dicloxacillin**, consider wound revision.

### Wings

Treat malaria according to local guidelines if testing positive.

Also consider meningitis (neck stiffness), cholecystitis (right upper abdominal pain) and appendicitis (right lower abdominal pain, or at the level of the umbilicus when pregnant). Start treatment and transfer to a higher-level facility.

Treat with **IV antibiotics** for 48 hours after the woman is fever free, then **oral antibiotics** for 5-7 days or more depending on the diagnosis.

Ensure that the HIV status of all women is known, and that appropriate antiretroviral therapy protocols are followed.

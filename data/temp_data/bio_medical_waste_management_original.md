# Chapter: Bio Medical Waste Management in COVID-19 isolation wards

Follow Bio Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 and Amendments.

Maintain Proper Segregation of Waste.

Use double layered bags (2 bags) for waste collection so as to ensure adequate strength.

All the plastic bags should be properly sealed, labelled and recorded before disposal.

Store waste in temporary storage room before handing over to CBWTF labelled with COVID-19.

General waste (not contaminated with COVID-19) should be disposed as Solid waste rules 2016.

Use dedicated trolley and collection bins for COVID-19 (should be labelled COVID19)

Inner and outer surface of trolley can be disinfected by 1% Sodium Hypochlorite solution daily.

## Colour coding

RED BIN - same as general and splash proof apron, gloves, plastic coverall, goggle and face shield (but it can be reused after decontamination)

YELLOW BIN - same as general, used testing kits, N-95 masks, triple layer mask, head cover/cap, shoe cover, disposable linen gown.

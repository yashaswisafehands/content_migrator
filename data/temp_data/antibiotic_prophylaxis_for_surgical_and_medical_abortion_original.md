# Chapter: Antibiotic prophylaxis for surgical and medical abortion

## Surgical abortion

For surgical abortion appropriate prophylactic antibiotics pre- or perioperatively is recommended.

Single-dose administration of nitroimidazoles, tetracyclines or penicillins has been shown to be effective when used as prophylactic antibiotics for surgical abortion.

Before the procedure you give a single dose of:

- Doxycycline 200mg orally, OR
- Azithromycin 500mg orally, OR
- Metronidazole 500mg orally

See drug list for details.

## Medical abortion

The use of prophylactic antibiotics for medical abortion is not recommended.

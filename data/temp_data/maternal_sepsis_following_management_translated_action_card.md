### Monitoring: Maternal sepsis

- Assess vital signs every half hour until stabilized.

- Intravenous fluid treatment is important. 
- Infuse normal saline or Ringer’s lactate 2-6 L over the next 12 hours to maintain a systolic blood pressure of ≥ 90 mmHg and an average urine output of ≥ 50 ml/hour.

- Assess the fluid balance:

- Record all oral and intravenous input.
- Record all urinary, faeces and vomiting output.
- Record perspiration output as 1.5 L/hour with fever and 1 L/hour without fever.

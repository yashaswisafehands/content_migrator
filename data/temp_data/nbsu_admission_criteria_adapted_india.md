# Chapter: NBSU Admission Criteria

- Newborns presenting to FRU/NBSU with emergency signs
- Newborns not having emergency signs, weight > 1800 gm and any of the following signs of sickness:

1. Feeding problem
2. Breathing Rate 60-70breaths /min
3. Hyperthermia (axillary temperature >37.5 °C)
4. Hypothermia (35.5°C -36.4°C)
5. Jaundice requiring only phototherapy
6. Newborns with suspected sepsis

- Weight 1500-1800 grams with no sign of sickness.
- Newborns who cannot be transferred to SNCU or referral facility due to any reason
- Newborns back-referred (from SNCU) to NBSU for completion of treatment.

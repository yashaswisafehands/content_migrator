# Chapter: Ambu Bag & Mask: Parts/Functions

## The parts of bag and mask are;

- Bag
- Oxygen inlet
- Air inlet
- Patient outlet
- Valve assembly
- Oxygen reservoir

![Image](05be5c8aa6a84c0fa0f9853b1686edd3)

## Bag:

- Resuscitation bags used for newborns are self-inflating bags that inflate automatically without a compressed gas source. 
- Recommended volume of bag is 240 to 500 ml. 
- It is made of silicone rubber, can be easily cleaned and autoclaved.
- Posterior part of bag consists of two inlets.

**Posterior part of the bag consist of two inlets:**

- The wider inlet is for air and the other one for oxygen. 
- Oxygen tubing is attached at the oxygen inlet and the oxygen reservoir, if used, is attached to the air inlet.

## Pop-off valve:

- It is situated on top of the bag. 
- It is a pressure release valve, which opens if excessive pressure is generated.
- Ventilate with high pressure and/or rate causes alveoli rupture and air leak.

## Mask:

- Mask is attached at the anterior end of bag. 
- It comes in different sizes and shapes (round and anatomically shaped).
- Anatomically shaped masks to fit contours of face to be placed on face with pointed part of mask fitting over nose. 
- Size of mask: for preterm (size 0) and term newborn (size 1)

## Safety feature:

There are two safety features:

1. Pressure release valve also called the pop-off valve, and
2. Pressure gauge

## How to check for bag functionality:

Assemble and check bag:

- By occluding the patient outlet tightly with the palm 
- Squeeze bag and look for release of pop-off valve
- Pop-off valve goes up along with a hissing sound indicating that the bag is functioning
- If the bag doesn’t generate pressure when tested, it may be due to:

1. Cracked bag
2. Leakage at air inlet
3. Pop-off valve defective
4. In such situation, change the bag

## Cleaning and disinfection of bag and mask

- Disassemble all parts, wash thoroughly with warm water and soap. 
- Soak in glutaraldehyde 2% (30 minutes for disinfection ,4-6 hours for sterilization).
- After removing from glutaraldehyde, rinse with sterile water, dry with sterile cloth and reassemble. 
- Disinfect daily and sterilize weekly.

# Chapter: Donning

## Preparation

Donning means to wear
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

It must be done outside the infectious ward in a specific donning designated area.

Health workers must be prepared by taking a meal, using washroom and water drinking before entering the donning areas.

Remove all the jewellery, watches, mobile phones etc and wear hospital scrub and washable foot ware.

Perform hand hygiene.

1. Wear head cap
2. Wear shoe cover
3. Wear the first pair of gloves
4. Wear the gown (look for any damage including tears, dirt)
5. Wear n 95 mask (Wear lower strap then upper strap, Make proper seal for air)
6. Wear eye goggle
7. Wear hood with strap crossing over each other
8. If face shield is available wear after wearing the hood
9. Wear 2nd pair of gloves

## How long can you wear your PPE?

- Surgical mask - 4-6 hours
- N-95 respirator - 6-8 hours
- Coverall / Hazmat suits - PPE - 6-8 hours 
- Any contamination or compromise - discard immediately

# Doffing

Doffing means taking off the PPE.

This should be done in a specific area. Check for any leaks before doffing and perform hand hygiene after every step. Follow BMW rules 2016 for discarding the PPE.

1. Clean any contamination on the gown. Use alcohol based wipes
2. Disinfect your outer gloves
3. Discard the shoe cover 
4. Disinfect your gloves 
5. Remove your glove from one hand then from the other making single bag of gloves
6. Remove hood first
7. Disinfect inner gloves
8. Remove gown 
9. Remove eye goggle. Do not touch outer surface
10. Disinfect your gloves
11. Remove inner pair of gloves as previously shown
12. Perform hand hygiene and wear new gloves before removing mask
13. Remove N 95 mask. Do not touch the exposed surface of mask. Lower strap then upper strap. 
14. Disinfect gloves
15. Disinfect outer surface of shoes with alcohol wipes
16. Disinfect your gloves again & remove your gloves to discard 
17. Perform hand hygiene
18. Exit the Doffing area and take shower.

NOTE: Donning exit area and doffing entry area should be separate.

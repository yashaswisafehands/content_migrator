# Chapter: Short Acting Reversible Contraceptives (SARCs)

![Image](03171dc5efde49e99bef910d89a99d85)

Condoms - can be used as soon as sexual activity resumes.

Combined Oral Contraceptives (COCs), Injectables (MPA – Medroxyprogesterone Acetate), Progesterone-only Pills (POPs)and Centchroman- can be used as follows:

### First Trimester:

- Start immediately or within 7 days using surgical method.
- With MMA: start same day as misoprostol or within 5 days.

### Second Trimester:

- Start immediately or within 7 days after abortion.

### Beyond 24 Weeks:

- Start 3 weeks after foetal expulsion.

# Chapter: Long Acting Reversible Contraceptives (LARCs)

![Image](923413c305994569ad114544ce441f64)

## 1. Implant

### First Trimester:

- Start immediately or within 7 days after vacuum aspiration.
- With MMA: can be given on day of misoprostol use.

### Second Trimester:

- Within 7 days after abortion.

### Beyond 24 Weeks:

- Anytime post-expulsion, within 3 weeks.

## 2. Intrauterine Contraceptive Device (IUCD)-Post abortion IUCD

### First Trimester Abortion:

- Insert immediately or within 12 days of complete abortion using surgical method.
- With MMA: around day 15 after confirming products of conception are expelled, and ruling out infection.

### Second Trimester Abortion:

- Insert immediately or within 12 days after complete abortion and ruling out contraindications.

### Beyond 24 Weeks:

- Insert within 48 hours after foetal expulsion or after 6 weeks.

# Chapter: Permanent Methods

## 1. Female Sterilization

### First Trimester:

- Can be done if no infection/severe blood loss concurrently or upto 7 days.
- With MMA: it can be done only after the first menstrual cycle.

### Second Trimester & Beyond 24 Weeks:

- Minilap can be done immediately or up to 7 days post-expulsion, if no infection/severe bleeding.
- Laparoscopic sterilization is not recommended.

## 2. Male Sterilization

- Can be done any time, independent of abortion.

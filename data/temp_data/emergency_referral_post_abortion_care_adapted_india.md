# Chapter: Post Abortion Care

### Emergency referral for post abortion care

> Emergency!

Do not refer if emergency is not controlled!

Never refer a woman with uncontrolled bleeding, un-evacuated septic or haemorrhagic abortion or septic shock.

After emergency management and when in a stable condition, discuss decision of referral with woman and relatives.

Quickly organize referral transport.

Inform the referral centre about woman’s condition, interventions performed and expected procedures to be done in facility.

Accompany the woman.

It is crucial that she is accompanied by a health care provider who is trained in emergency management, if it resurrects during transport

Consider to bring a relative who can donate blood.

### Bring

Referral slip including history, physical examination, treatment and reason for referral

## Essential emergency drugs:

- **Ampicillin**,**Gentamicin**and  **Metronidazole**for infections

## Supplies:

- IV equipment + IV fluids+ Syringes of different sizes
- Blood pressure monitor
- Adult Ambu bag with appropriate mask size
- Oxygen cylinder

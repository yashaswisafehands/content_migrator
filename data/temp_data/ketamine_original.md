# Chapter: Ketamine

> WARNING: Do not use Ketamine for women with hypertension, pre-eclampsia/eclampsia!

## Indications and usage

Ketamine (Ketamine hydrochloride) is a dissociative analgesia. It is used as general analgesia to prevent pain and discomfort during medical procedures, or minor surgery. It can be used as an anaesthetic for manual removal of placenta or placental tissue.

When used alone, ketamine can cause unpleasant hallucinations. 
To prevent hallucinations, give diazepam 10 mg IV after the baby is delivered

## Dosage and Administration

2 mg/kg IV slowly over 2 minutes.

## Contraindications

Elevated blood pressure, pre-eclampsia, eclampsia or heart disease.

Avoid use in women with a history of psychosis.

## Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are blurred vision, dizziness, drowsiness, nausea, vomiting, and loss of appetite.

Hallucinations, vivid dreams, floating sensations and delirium.

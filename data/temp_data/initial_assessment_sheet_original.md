# Chapter: What is Initial Assessment Sheet (IAS)?

### What is Initial Assessment Sheet (IAS)?

## IAS is a tool to:

1. Observe the three conditions (of women, fetus, and delivery progress), when a woman visit HC for delivery 
2. Conduct adequate assessment on the conditions to distinguish: ‘normal (green)’ ‘risk to be complicated (yellow)’ ‘abnormal (red)’ 
3. Identify and refer ‘real’ emergency pregnant woman (‘abnormal (red)’) with proper first aid 
4. Take feasible necessary actions for ‘normal (green)’ or ‘risk to be complicated (yellow)’

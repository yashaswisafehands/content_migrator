# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Antihypertensive drug used for example at severe pre- eclampsia and acute hypertensive crisis.

**CAUTION:**Diastolic blood pressure should not be lowered below 90 mm Hg to avoid effect on foetal circulation.

# Dosage and Administration

5 mg IV slowly (1-2 minutes) Repeat 5 to 10 milligrams over 2 minutes after 20 minutes.

Give maximum 20 mg/24 hours.

It is important that before delivery the diastolic blood pressure should not be lowered below 90 mmHg, otherwise the blood supply to the foetus might be compromised.

# Contraindications

Tachycardia, lupus erythematosus disseminatus, and rheumatoid arthritis.

# Adverse Reactions

Common adverse reactions are headache, flushing, tachycardia, allergic reactions and abdominal pain.

Uncommon adverse reactions are nose congestion, feeling disorders, increased tear flow, and tremor.

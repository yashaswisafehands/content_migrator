# Chapter: Management of Eclampsia

### Immediate Management

- Keep women in bed with padded rails on the sides, preferably near the nursing station
- Position her on the left side 
- Keep oropharyngeal airway patent.
- Do oronasal suction to remove secretions 
- Put airway and catheterize bladder
- Ensure preparedness to manage maternal and foetal complications 
- Give oxygen by mask at 6 - 8 L/min
- Start IV fluids - RL/NS @ 75ml/hr

1. If at a peripheral facility, give prereferral dose: 50% Magnesium Sulphate - 5 grams in each buttock deep IM and refer the women.
2. At all other facilities, give Magnesium Sulphate in two steps:

### Maintenance Dose of Magnesium Sulphate

- Give 5 gram 50% of magnesium sulphate deep Intramuscular every 4 hours in alternate buttock after monitoring.
- Monitor Toxicity before giving each maintenance dose of magnesium sulphate.
- Continue maintenance dose till 24 hours to after last fit or delivery whichever is later

### Magnesium Sulphate - Toxicity Signs

Before giving each maintenance dose of MgSO4, monitor the following:

- Presence of Patellar jerk 
- Respiratory Rate (RR) >16 breaths/min 
- Urine output ≥30ml/hr in last 4 hours

If patellar jerk is absent and urine output <30ml/hr withhold MgSo4 and monitor hourly

Restart maintenance dose when criteria are fulfilled.

> If RR <16 breaths/min

- Withhold MgSO4
- **Give antidote - Calcium Gluconate 1 gm IV**(10 ml of 10% solution) in 10 minutes

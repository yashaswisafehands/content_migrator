### Definition of septic abortion

**Septic abortion** is diagnosed by the following signs:

- Tender uterus and

![Image](0aa5c5f3356f40aea8fe4d6ac9aaace5)

- Signs of severe peritonitis (such as rebound tenderness) or

![Image](65c36f6112834a1487d947876f7b8dbf)

- Abnormal foul smelling vaginal discharge or

![Image](580776c1252347268c4f5a3800830070)

- Excessive vaginal bleeding

![Image](acad274196164d0fb280d88ca1847429)

- Any signs of shock (systolic blood pressure below 90mmHg) or pre-shock (pulse above 110bpm)

![Image](2f478309fd7047398ae57baa5c1deb96)

> OR

> Fever > 38° C

Septic abortion often, but not always, causes fever. A woman with sepsis can have an increased, normal, or low temperature.

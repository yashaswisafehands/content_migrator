# Chapter: Follow-up visits

### Follow-up visits:

Women with gestational hypertension or pre-eclampsia without severe symptoms should be seen once or twice a week until 37 weeks, when birth is recommended.  

Medication dosage (e.g. methyldopa) should be regularly evaluated to ensure appropriate management.

Advise the women to seek medical care with any danger signs, such as headaches, visual disturbances, dizziness, nausea, and epigastric pain.

## At each visit you should check:

- Blood pressure
- Proteinuria (urinalysis/urine for protein) 
- Weight of the mother
- Signs of generalized edema
- Monitor the fetal condition (foetal weight estimate, symphysial-fundal height, foetal movement and foetal heart rate)
- Respiratory rate (if above 22 per minute, admit)
- Reflexes
- Presence of danger signs (see above)

Women with gestational hypertension or pre-eclampsia without severe symptoms, do not need to be admitted if they can be seen once or twice a week and they can access care immediately, should they develop severe symptoms.

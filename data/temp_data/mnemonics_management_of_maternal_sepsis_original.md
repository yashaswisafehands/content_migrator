### Call a CAB – initial response

- **C**irculation – put up 2 IV lines with **2 liters** of **Ringer’s lactate**/ **normal saline** and run it fast
- **A**irway – ensure airway is open
- **B**reathing – give an oxygen mask

### Secondary Survey

## Big 5

1. CNS – level of consciousness, neck stiffness
2. CVS – BP & P every 15 minutes
3. Respiration – listen to lungs (pneumonia), monitor respiratory rate and **oxygen** saturation, chest X ray
4. Liver and GIT – look for jaundice, listen for bowel signs, take blood for AST (appendicitis, cholangitis etc.)
5. Renal system- insert catheter, monitor urine output hourly, take blood for urea and creatinine, send urine for culture

## Forgotten 4

1. Haematological system– take blood for haemoglobin, platelets, white cell count and clotting profile if chocked + malaria smear
2. Endocrine system – measure blood glucose
3. Immunological system– HIV-status + TP
4. Musculoskeletal system – cellulitis, DVTs

## Core 1

- Assess abdomen for tenderness, peritonitis and uterine size
- Do vaginal examination – speculum and bimanual examination + look for signs of infection

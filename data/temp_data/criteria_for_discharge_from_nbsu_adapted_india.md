# Chapter: When to discharge from NBSU:

1. Newborn maintains normal body temperature (in room temperature / when care from by mother)
2. Newborn not requiring IV fluids/medications.
3. Newborn is accepting breastfeeds/assisted feeds well and gaining weight for 3 consecutive days.
4. Newborn admitted for phototherapy no more requires it.
5. Counselled mother for danger signs, assisted feeding ,KMC if required, and plan for follow up.

# Chapter: Preparation for Discharge

1. Prepare discharge summary. The birth weight, discharge weight, length and head circumference should be mentioned in the discharge summary. Suggested format provided in annexure.
2. Also inform ASHAs/ANM about the discharge and home based follow up
3. Newborn should have received the following vaccines- BCG, zero dose OPV (can be given till 14th day of life) and birth dose of hepatitis B (within 24 hours of birth). 
4. Advice screening for preterm babies at higher centres for screening (* ROP (Retinopathy of prematurity) or all newborns <2000g within first month of life. * BERA (Brainstem evoked response audiometry) at 40 weeks corrected age

While explaining the discharge instruction to mother:

1. Use words that she understands
2. Use teaching aids that are familiar to her
3. Give feedback when she practices. Praise what was done well and make corrections if necessary
4. Allow more practice, if needed
5. Encourage the parent/mother to ask questions and answer all her questions.
6. **Advise mother to give home care : Breastfeed infant exclusively, keep infant warm, apply nothing to cord, handwashing & danger signs.**

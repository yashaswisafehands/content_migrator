# Chapter: BMI calculation and recommended weight gain in pregnancy

## Body Mass Index (BMI) calculation and recommended weight gain during pregnancy

BMI calculation helps in determining the percentage of desirable pre-pregnancy body weight and optimal pattern of weight gain during pregnancy.

- Calculation: BMI =Weight (kg) / (Height in m)^2 
- i.e.: Weight of a woman is 60 kg and height is 155 cm.
- BMI = 60 / (1.55)2 = 60 / 2.40 = 25 
- **BMI of the woman is 25.**

Weight gain in pregnancy is recommended in relation to the pre-pregnancy body mass index.

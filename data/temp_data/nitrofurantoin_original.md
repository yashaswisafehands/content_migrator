# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Cystitis.

If treatment fails or if infection recurs 2 or more times during pregnancy, check urine culture and sensitivity, if available, and treat with an appropriate antibiotic.

# Dosage and Administration

Nitrofurantoin 100 mg orally every 8 hours for 3 days.

# Contraindications

Avoid nitrofurantoin at term as it can cause neonatal haemolysis (instead give amoxicillin 500 mg orally).

Oliguria or reduced kidney function.

Porphyria.

Previous severe adverse reactions to nitrofurantoin.

It should be avoided in the last weeks of pregnancy as there is a slightly increased risk of haemolytic anaemia in the newborn.

It should also be avoided for the breastfeeding premature or asphyxiated newborn, as it concentrates in milk and commonly causes diarrhoea in the newborn.

# Adverse reactions

Common adverse reactions are reduced appetite, vomiting, diarrhoea and skin rash.

Uncommon adverse reactions are fever, respiratory distress, headache, dizziness, urticaria and change of colour of the urine.

Rare adverse reactions are hepatitis, anaphylactic reactions, affection of lungs and pancreas, thrombocytopenia and neuropathy.

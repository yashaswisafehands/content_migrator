# Chapter: IUCD insertion introduction

Interval IUCD can be inserted at any time in the menstrual cycle if it is certain the woman is not pregnant and does not have sexually transmitted infections like chlamydia or gonorrhoea.

It can be inserted within 48 hours following a birth (including caesarean section). If more than 48, delay until 6 weeks postnatal. Post abortion intrauterine contraceptive device (PAIUCD)  can also be inserted immediately after a surgical abortion, and after ensuring the passage of products of conception in medical abortion.

## Documentation

Check woman’s record for eligibility. Ensure that she is counselled and informed consent is being obtained. Fill and give a IUCD card to the woman with the following information:

- Type of IUCD  (380 A/ 375)  IUD/IUS and date of insertion 
- Timing of insertion- Interval, postpartum (vaginal delivery), postpartum (intracesarean), post-abortion
- Date when the IUCD IUD/IUS will need to be removed or replaced
- Where to go if any   she has any problem/complaints and information on post follow-up advise.
- Warning signs for woman’s information.

# Chapter: Postpartum intrauterine contraceptive device (PPIUCD) insertion

## Sterile equipment

- Sterile tray 
- Ring/ Sponge holding forceps
- Sim’s speculum 
- PPIUCD insertion forceps
- Bowl with cotton balls 
- Sterile Gloves

![Image](1d4f740f5b27459cadb79e5c7a024031)

## Other supplies

- Headlight or surgical lamp 
- Antiseptic solution
- IUCD 380 A or IUCD 375 in a sealed pack.

## Insertion procedure

1. Check for any delivery related contraindications (e.g., chorioamnionitis, prolonged rupture of membranes (>18 hours), unresolved postpartum haemorrhage, puerperal sepsis, extensive genital trauma) 
2. The PPIUCD is inserted after removal of placenta 
3. Confirm if the woman had been counselled and given her consent. Wash hands and wear sterile gloves.
4. Clean the cervix and vagina with antiseptic solution atleast 2 times and wait for 2 minutes. 
5. Using a speculum, to visualize the anterior cervix
6. Gently grasp the anterior lip of the cervix with ring forceps and leave it gently. 
7. Prepare for IUD/IUS insertion. Opens sterile package of IUCD by pulling plastic cover approximately 1/3 upwards
8. Hold the IUCD over the package and stabilize it. Remove plunger rod and inserter tube from the package.
9. Grasp IUCD with PPIUCD forceps using no-touch technique.
10. Hold the IUCD at the edge of the PPIUCD forceps for easy release
11. Gently lift anterior lip of cervix using ring forceps and apply gentle traction to steady the cervix. 
12. Insert IUCD into lower uterine cavity and gently remove the ring forceps once slight resistance is felt.
13. Release the hand that was retracting the cervix and place it on the abdomen.
14. Stabilize the uterus with hand, adding light upward pressure to straighten the curvature between vagina and uterus
15. Advance the PPIUCD forceps to the uterine fundus, keeping it closed.
16. Confirm fundal placement with both abdominal hand and the inserting hand by feeling the resistance of the forceps at the uterine fundus.
17. Release the IUCD from the forceps by tilting slightly towards midline. 
18. Withdraw the forceps along the uterine side wall, keeping it open and the uterus stabilized. 
19. Ensure there is no bleeding and check if IUCD or strings are visible outside the cervix.

# Chapter: Interval IUCD insertion

## Sterile equipment

- Sterile tray
- Sterile Gloves 
- Ring/ Sponge holding forceps
- Tenaculum or Vulsellum
- Uterine Sound
- Anterior vaginal wall retractor
- Scissors
- Bowl with cotton balls

![Image](2ed02f12f6714451a4fa08fe6ee474fc)

## Other supplies

- Headlight or surgical lamp
- Antiseptic solution
- IUCD 380 A or IUCD  375 in a sealed pack.

## Insertion procedure

1. Check woman’s records for eligibility and confirm that the woman is counselled and obtain informed consent.
2. Ask the woman to empty her bladder and wash the perineal area. Wash hands and put on sterile gloves. Do a pelvic examination to determine the uterine size, position of the uterus as well as tenderness.
3. Do bimanual examination   and gently insert speculum into the vagina to inspect the cervix.
4. Clean the cervix and vaginal wall with antiseptic.
5. Gently grasps the anterior lip of the cervix with a vulsellum or tenaculum at 11 o’clock or 1 o’clock position and apply gentle traction to hold the cervix and uterus steady.
6. Confirm the uterine size and position by passing the uterine sound through the cervix using “no-touch” technique.
7. Load IUCD 380A in its sterile package using the ‘no-touch technique’ and adjust the blue length gauge to uterine length. Loading is not required for IUCD 375.
8. Insert the loaded IUCD carefully into the uterine cavity.
9. Withdraw the insertion  tube downwards , while keeping the vulsellum and plunger rod fixed to release the IUCD into the uterus (withdrawal technique).
10. Remove  the plunger rod. 
11. Move the insertion tube gently up to the fundus to ensure fundal placement of the IUCD 
12. Hold vulsellum and partially withdraw the insertion tube until the strings can be seen extending from the cervical os.
13. Cut strings leaving about 3 -4 cm hanging out of the cervix
14. Gently remove the vulsellum, check for bleeding, then remove the speculum.
15. Advise follow-up after 6 weeks or the next menstruation, whichever is earlier

# Chapter: पोस्ट- इंसर्शन फॉलो– अप

Ask if the woman if she is satisfied with the method and if she has questions. Ask her if she has experienced any warning signs:

**PAINS**

- **P:** Period related problems or pregnancy symptoms
- **A:** Abdominal pain or pain during intercourse
- **I:** Infections or unusual vaginal discharge
- **N:** Not feeling well, fever, chills
- **S:**String problems

**Note-**Perform pelvic examination for first checkup to confirm IUCD placement and check for signs of infection. For subsequent follow-ups, perform a pelvic exam only if infection is suspected.

# Chapter: The non-touch technique

## IUCD insertion

During IUCD insertion, a no-touch technique means not letting the loaded IUCD or uterine sound touch any unsterile surface (hands, speculum, vagina, tabletop etc.), and involves:

- Loading the IUCD 380 A  in  its sterile package, to avoid direct contact while inserting Interval IUCD or PAIUCD (less than 12 weeks abortion).
- Cleaning the cervix thoroughly with antiseptic before insertion
- Being careful not to touch the vaginal wall or speculum blades with the uterine sound or loaded IUCD 
- Passing the uterine sound and loaded IUCD only once each through the cervical canal 
- During PPIUCD insertion, grasps IUCD with PPIUCD forceps in the sterile package to avoid direct contact.

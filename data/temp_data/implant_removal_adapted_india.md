# Chapter: Implant removal

Implants should be removed without delay at a woman’s request, regardless of where she is in her menstrual cycle. Her fertility will return immediately following removal. Confirm the woman's desire to remove the implant and explain the procedure.

## Sterile equipment

- Sterile tray 
- Ring/ Sponge holding forceps 
- Mosquito forceps - Straight 
- Mosquito forceps - Curved 
- Bowl with cotton balls 
- Sterile scalpel with 11 number blade 
- Sterile Gauze 
- Sterile cut sheet
- Sterile Gloves
- New implant (in case of concurrent reinsertion)

## Other supplies:

- Marker pen 
- Measuring scale
- Antiseptic solution 
- Pressure Bandage
- 1% Lignocaine Syringe with needle (22 gauge)
- Surgical adhesive tape

## Removal procedure

1. Check if woman’s arm is clean, if not, ask woman to wash with soap and water and dry it.
2. Help the woman to lie on her back with arm flexed at elbow and hand behind her head.
3. Gently locate the implant and mark the distal end closest to the elbow 
4. Do not attempt to remove the implant until its exact location is identified
5. Wash hands and wear sterile gloves.
6. Clean the skin with antiseptic solution 
7. Inject 0.5 to 1 ml of 1% lignocaine a small amount of local anaesthetic below the distal end of the implant and wait 2 minutes 
8. Make a shallow 4 2 mm longitudinal incision at lower palpable end of implant, keeping the skin flat
9. Gently push the implant towards the incision with your fingers and use mosquito forceps to grasp it when the tip of it becomes visible
10. If the implant is encapsulated, gently use the scalpel to open the fibrous tissue capsule around the implant rod.
11. Applying light pressure on the other end of the implant should cause it to ‘pop’ out of the capsule.
12. Grasp the exposed implant with 2nd pair of mosquito forceps and pull out the implant rod gently
13. If the implant tip is not visible, insert curved mosquito forceps into the incision, grasp and lift it towards the woman’s shoulder, then use a second pair of forceps to separate surrounding tissue and remove the implant. 
14. Ensure that the removed implant is complete and show it to the client. If the woman needs a new implant, it can be inserted into the same location or to a different area if preferred.
15. Check for bleeding. Apply antiseptic, put on sterile dressing and wrap with pressure bandage.
16. If the implant can’t be grasped, stop the procedure and refer the woman to an experienced professional or higher facility.

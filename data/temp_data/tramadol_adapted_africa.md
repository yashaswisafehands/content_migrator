# Chapter: Indication and usage

Tramadol, a centrally acting Opioid analgesic, is used to treat moderate to (moderately) severe pain before Manual Vacuum Aspiration.

# Dosage and administration

30 minutes before procedure:

Administration: IM

Dose: 50 mg

# Contraindications

Severe asthma or breathing problems, a blockage in the stomach or intestines, intoxication with alcohol, use of sedatives, tranquilizers, narcotic medication, or an MAO inhibitor.

# Adverse reactions

Adverse reactions include seizures, nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite, dizziness, tiredness or weakness.

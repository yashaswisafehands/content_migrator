# Chapter: Indications and Usage

Tranexamic acid is an antifibrinolytic.

It is used for treatment of cyclic heavy menstrual bleeding in females.

It works by preventing blood clots from breaking down too quickly.

This helps to reduce excessive bleeding.

# Dosage and Administration

Administer 1 gm Tranexamic Acid slowly IV in 10 ml NS IV over 10 mins (100 mg/min).

**Continuing dose:**

Repeat after 30 minutes of first dose if bleeding continues.

**Maximum dose:**

Not more than 10 mg per kg body weight, three to four times daily.

# Contraindications

Allergy to Tranexamic Acid

Intracranial bleeding or risk of clot

If person has defective colour vision 

History of thromboembolism or active thromboembolic disease

# Adverse Reactions

COMMON side effects:

Back pain, headache, joint pain, muscle pain, spasms, or cramps, nasal or sinus congestion, stomach pain, tiredness.

SEVERE and RARE side effects:

Severe allergic reactions (rash, hives, itching, difficulty breathing, tightness in the chest or throat, swelling of the mouth, face, lips, or tongue, flushing of the face), calf or leg pain, swelling, or tenderness, confusion, coughing up blood, decreased urination or difficulty urinating, eye problems, fainting, numbness of an arm or leg, one-sided weakness, seizures, severe or persistent dizziness or light-headedness, slurred speech, sudden, severe headache or vomiting, unusual tiredness or weakness, vision changes or problems (e.g. disturbance of colour vision, sharpness, or field of vision).

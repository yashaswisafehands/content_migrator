### Uterine Balloon Tamponade

### Indication

Uterine or Condom Balloon Tamponade is a form of intrauterine tamponade to alleviate or stop profuse bleeding especially during atonic uterus.

### Materials Required

- IV-fluids, IV fluid set, IV stand
- Sterile gloves
- Condom
- Sterile suture
- Foleys catheter and uro bag
- Sponge holder -1
- Artery forceps -1
- Sims /Cuscos speculum-1
- Light

###

- Sterile Condom 
- Sterile string 
- IV fluid set 
- Sterile long ring forceps  
- Sterile artery clamp 
- Catheter – (Foley’s or similar)
- Sterile gloves 
- IV fluid of any kind (500 ml or 1 L)
- Sims speculum 
- 60 ml syringe 
- Antibiotics: Inj. ampicillin OR cefazolin 
- Marker/ pen

### Preparation for the procedure

Prepares all necessary equipment

Tell the woman what is going to be done, answer her doubts and questions.

![Image](fb0a3cc4f1fc4a438091ca35ae95b70c)

Ensure the bladder is empty. Catheterize the woman if necessary.

Give prophylactic antibiotics.

Place the condom over the catheter and roll it over the catheter until the condom is completely unrolled. Securely tie the end of the condom with the sterile string.

### Procedure

Wash hands and wear sterile gloves.

Tie the lower end of condom snugly on Foley’s catheter using sterile suture.

Take care that tie should be tight enough to prevent leakage of saline solution but should not prevent flow of fluid.

Clean the vulva.

Insert the sims speculum in posterior vaginal wall.

Holds the cervix with the Ring forceps.

Use the artery forceps to place the condom’s end high into uterine cavity.

![Image](e17adbb2316447a6a1758d755f37ef91)

Connect outlet of Foley’s catheter to IV set which has been connected to infusion bag.

Inflate condom with saline with 300 -500 ml.

![Image](41c9134487884bfab64abec72959ba5c)

Clamp the end of catheter when desired volume is achieved, or bleeding is controlled.

Maintain it in-situ for 12- 24 hours if bleeding is controlled and woman is stable

Mark the level of the uterine fundus.

Monitor if the fundus rises beyond the mark.

This is a sign of hidden bleeding. **Be alert!**

If bleeding is not controlled 15 minutes after inserting the balloon tamponade, keep the device in place and transport the woman for surgical care immediately.

If bleeding is controlled, administer **oxytocin 20 units in 1 litre** of IV fluid at 60 drops per minute.

Administer a single dose of antibiotics to prevent infection ‒ **either ampicillin 2 grams IV OR cefazolin 1 gram IV.**

### Post-procedure care

Removes gloves and wash hands

Monitor vital signs, uterine fundal height, and vaginal bleeding every 30 minutes.

The condom can be kept inside the uterus for days if transport is needed.

Places a mark on the abdomen at the level of uterine fundus. Any increase in uterine size above this mark, along with changes in vital signs, suggests blood is accumulating within the uterine cavity above the tamponade.

Documents the procedure and all parameter in case record.

If bleeding is not controlled within 15 minutes of initial insertion of uterine tamponade, abandon the procedure and plan surgical intervention.

<color style="blue">**When to deflate?**</color>

When bleeding is controlled and woman is stable for at least 12 hours, slowly deflates the condom by letting out 200 ml of saline every hour.

Re-inflates it to the previous level if bleeding reoccurs and, consider surgical intervention if bleeding doesn’t control.

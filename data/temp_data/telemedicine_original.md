# Chapter: Telemedicine

Telemedicine or Telehealth is a mode of health service delivery where providers can interact with clients at a distance using various technologies.

The interaction may take place in real time (e.g., by telephone or video link) or asynchronously (e.g., by email or text/voice/audio message).

**Telemedicine can be used as an alternative to in-person interactions with the health worker to completely or partially deliver medical abortion services.**

Telemedicine can allow health workers to assess eligibility for a medical abortion, provide counselling and/or directions related to the abortion process and drug administrations, and carry out follow-up post-abortion care.

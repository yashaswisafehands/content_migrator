# Chapter: Indication and usage

Misoprostol is a prostaglandin E1 analogue that can be used as cervical softening and dilation and uterine contraction. 

Misoprostol is an effective cervical ripening agent before first-trimester surgical abortion.

# Dosage and Administration

Prepare the cervix by giving:

Misoprostol 400 mcg either (vaginally, 2-3 hours prior to the procedure) OR sublingually, (1-2 hours prior to the procedure).

Alternatively, administer mifepristone 200 mg orally 24-48 hours prior to the procedure.

# Contraindications

Previous allergic reaction to misoprostol

Known or suspected ectopic pregnancy

# Adverse reactions:

1 in 3 treated with misoprostol will have diarrhea or abdominal pain.

It is also common to see a non-allergic rash.

A few will experience a transient moderate increase in temperature.

Some will have nausea, vomiting, constipation or flatulence.

Less common: headaches and dizziness.

Rarely Misoprostol can cause liver damage.

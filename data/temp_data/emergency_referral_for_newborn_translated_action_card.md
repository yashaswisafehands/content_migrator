# Chapter: Emergency Referral

### नियोनेटल ट्रांसपोर्ट

>

जो नवजात शिशु गंभीर रूप से बीमार हों और जिनका इलाज एनबीएसयू में संभव न हो, उन्हें एसएनसीयू में रेफर और ट्रांसफर किया जाना चाहिए।

समय पर ट्रांसपोर्ट की व्यवस्था करें और ट्रांसफर के दौरान नवजात शिशु को आवश्यक देखभाल अवश्य प्रदान करें।

If a newborn needs to be transferred to a higher-level facility: administer the recommended antibiotics prior to transfer.

Before and during transfer, it is important to keep the newborn warm. Use either skin-to-skin contact or wrap in a soft, dry cloth and blanket, ensuring that the head is covered.

Initiate breastfeeding or provide alternative breastmilk feeding.

Inform the referral centre.

Accompany the mother with the newborn.

It is crucial that the newborn is accompanied by a healthcare professional who is trained in managing an emergency.

Quickly organize transport and possible financial aid.

Consider bringing a relative who can donate blood.

###

Referral note.

##

- **Oxytocin** and **ergometrine** (alternatively **misoprostol**) for treatment of post partum haemorrhage
- **Hydralazine** for IV treatment of elevated blood pressure
- **Magnesium sulphate** for eclampsia
- **Calcium gluconate** for intoxication with **magnesium sulphate**
- **Ampicillin, gentamycin**and **metronidazole**for infections
- **Diazepam** for pain relief
- **Phenobarbital** for the newborn in case of convulsions
- **Adrenaline** for the newborn in case of cardiac arrest

##

- Towel
- IV-equipment + IV fluids
- Neonatal ambu bag with appropriate mask size
- Suction bulb
- Syringes of different sizes
- Examination and sterile gloves

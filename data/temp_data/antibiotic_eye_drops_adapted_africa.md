# Chapter: Indications and Usage

For prophylaxis of ophthalmic neonatorum due to N. gonorrhoea or C. trachomatis.

Sexually transmitted infections (STIs), including gonorrhoea and chlamydia are a main cause of newborn eye infections (the infection can be transmitted to the newborn during the birth).

These infections can cause blindness if not treated.

Give antibiotic eye drops or ointment (e.g. erythromycin ophthalmic ointment) to the newborn to prevent eye infections.

# Dosage and Administration

**For prophylaxis**of neonatal gonococcal or chlamydial ophthalmia, a ribbon of ointment approximately 1 cm long should be instilled into each lower conjunctival sac.

The ointment should not be flushed out of the eyes following instillation.

A new tube should be used for each newborn.

**For treatment of eye infection:**

- Wash hands with clean water and soap.
- Wet a clean cloth with boiled and cooled water.
- Use a wet cloth to gently wash off pus from the newborn's eyes.
- Apply 1% tetracycline eye ointment in each eye 3 times daily.
- Wash hands.

# Contraindications

None

# Adverse Reactions

The most frequently reported adverse reactions are minor ocular irritations, redness and hypersensitivity reactions.

# Chapter: Medical Nutrition Therapy and Meal plan for GDM

## Meal composition

- Recommended meal composition is: 50%-60% calories from carbohydrate, 10-20% from protein, 25-30% from fat.
- Select carbohydrate foods which do not increase the blood glucose level instantly. 
- A mixed meal consisting of carbohydrates, protein, fat, fiber eaten together results in a slow blood sugar rise.

## Tips for planning meals for

- Follow discipline regarding meal timings.
- Avoid heavy meals, skipping meals or fasting for long hours. 
- Include all food groups in the daily diet like cereal, pulses, milk and milk products, fruits, vegetables, and fats. 
- For non-vegetarians include eggs, low fat meat like well-cooked fish or chicken.
- Divide meal plan into 3 major meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) and 2-3 mid-day snacks.

## Breakfast

- Start the day with a healthy breakfast and never skip it.
- Take 1-2 carbohydrate servings (like chapati/dalia/sandwich/poha/idli etc.) along with one serving of protein rich foods (like milk/curd/paneer/egg etc.)

## Lunch/dinner

- In lunch and dinner, the thali/plate can be divided into 4 parts.
- First ½ portion should be vegetables, Next ¼ portion protein rich food and next ¼ portion should be carbohydrates.
- Ensure at least 1 serving of low fat, sugar-free yoghurt, curd, or milk.
- Add 1 serving of fruits like guava, apple, berries, or any citrus fruits.
- Vegetables provide fiber which helps in controlling post prandial sugar level.

## Mid-day snack

- Maintain a 2-3-hour gap with major meal. 
- Avoid eating fried foods or junk foods or food with high amount of free sugars. 
- Options for snack: murmura chat, sprouts, vegetable dalia, vegetable poha, idli, vegetable uttapam, besan chilla with less amount of oil etc.

## General tips

- Avoid fried foods. Prefer to steam, boil or sauté food in less oil.
- Choose whole fruits over juices.
- Choose fish or chicken over red or organ meat.
- Increase fiber - include salad, beans, non-starchy vegetables, whole fruit, whole grain cereals/millets, whole pulses.
- Drink water, buttermilk, home-made soups, soya milk, sattu and other unsweetened healthy beverages instead of soda or fruit juices.

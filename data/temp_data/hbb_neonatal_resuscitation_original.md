# Chapter: Grey: Preparation for birth

### Preparation for birth

- Identify a helper and review the emergency plan
- Prepare the area for delivery
- Wash hands
- Prepare an area for ventilation
- Assemble disinfected equipment and supplies
- Test the ventilation bag, mask and suction supplies
- Prepare a uterotonic for the mother

# Chapter: Green: Routine Care

### Routine Care

- Dry thoroughly
- Recognize crying
- Keep warm
- Check breathing
- Clamp or tie and cut the umbilical cord
- Position on mother's chest to encourage breastfeeding
- Continue with essential newborn care, identify the baby, and complete the birth record

# Chapter: Yellow: The Golden Minute 

### The Golden Minute

![Image](3ca3c28f884345ddb5566ded246a9f92)

- Call out the time of birth
- Dry thoroughly
- Recognize not crying and not breathing
- Keep warm
- Clear airway if needed (or if born with meconium)
- Stimulate breathing by rubbing the back
- Recognize not breathing
- Follow routine for when to clamp or tie and cut the umbilical cord
- Move to area for ventilation, stand at head, assure appropriate mask size
- Ventilate (by 1 minute) at a rate of 40 breaths per minute. Count aloud "1...2...3...1...2...3"
- Ventilate for a full minute with the chest moving unless the baby begins to breathe

# Chapter: Red: Continued ventilation with normal or slow heart rate

### Continued ventilations with normal or slow heart rate

- Recognize not breathing and chest not moving
- Call for help
- Cut the cord if not already done
- Continue and improve ventilation (reapply mask, reposition head, clear mouth and nose of secretions, open mouth slightly, squeeze the bag harder)
- Recognize not breathing
- Recognize normal OR slow heart rate
- Recognize breathing OR not breathing
- If breathing and normal heart rate (>100 bpm) monitor with mother OR
- If not breathing or slow heart rate (<100 bpm) continue ventilation and decide on advanced care
- Communicate with the mother and family
- Continue with essential newborn care, identify the baby, complete the birth record, and review your actions
- Disinfect equipment

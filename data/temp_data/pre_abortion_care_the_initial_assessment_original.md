# Chapter: Pre-abortion care: the initial assessment

When a woman is seeking abortion care at your health facility, make sure to welcome her by making her comfortable and explain what will be involved during the visit.

![Image](c46a623e42304dd6b68159f3c2b9e85a)

Record the woman’s medical history.

Ask her about:

- **Pregnancy symptoms**, complications and vaginal bleeding, pelvic pain/cramping. 
- **Obstetric history**: previous pregnancies and their outcomes (including ectopic pregnancy, miscarriage or abortion, foetal deaths, live births, mode of delivery).
- **Gynaecological history**: first date of LMP, menstrual cycle history and pattern, FGM, contraceptives, bleeding tendencies/ disorders.
- **Sexual history**: current partner(s), history of and symptoms of STDs, HIV status.
- **Surgical/medical history:** chronical diseases, past hospitalisations, past surgical operations. 
- **Physical or cognitive disability,** including mental health conditions.
- **Medications and Allergies**: current and recent medications, herbal remedies, details of use, dose, route, timing. Allergies to any medications.
- **Social history:** partner status, sexual coercion and physical violence, use of alcohol and drugs, other social issues.

**Note:** You may encounter women with complicated social situations. Facilitating referral to services to meet women’s needs is important aspect of quality abortion care.

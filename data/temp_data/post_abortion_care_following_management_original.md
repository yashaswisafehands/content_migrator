### Monitoring after septic abortion

1. Monitor vital signs and fluid balance closely until stable.
2. Treat with **IV** **antibiotics**until 48 hours after the woman is fever-free, then **oral** **antibiotics for 5-7 days**.
3. Before discharge, advise on contraceptive methods and check for sexually transmitted diseases and HIV/AIDS.
4. Tell the woman to return in the case of feeling unwell (for example with fever), increasing lower abdominal pain, and with foul smelling discharge.

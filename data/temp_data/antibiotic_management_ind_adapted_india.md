# Chapter: Pre-Referral Dose of Antibiotics

The antibiotics of choice before transport are ampicillin and gentamicin.

![Image](7a4842801ab74faea71b985885d1ea76)

<color style="blue">***Determine if the child can take orally**</color>

Administer one dose of both antibiotics to all newborns who present with:

- Emergency signs 
- Suspected sepsis 
- Hypoglycaemia 
- Abdominal distension

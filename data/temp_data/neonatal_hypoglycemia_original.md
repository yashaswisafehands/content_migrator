# Chapter: Neonatal hypoglycemia

## Neonatal hypoglycemia

Newborns of GDM mothers are at risk of developing hypoglycemia.

Observe the newborn closely and check for hypoglycemia between 1 to 2 hours of birth by glucometer.

## Diagnosis of hypoglycemia

- Normal newborn - capillary blood sugar level < 45 mg/dL
- Intra uterine growth restriction (IUGR) newborn - capillary blood sugar level < 54 mg/dL

![Image](83dbb4c9526d4c1ca590502298db9328)

## Recognition of hypoglycemia

Most newborns may not have any signs of hypoglycemia.

However, observe for the following signs of hypoglycemia:

- Stupor or apathy
- Jitteriness or tremors
- Episodes of cyanosis
- Convulsions
- Intermittent apnoeic spells or tachypnoea
- Weak and high-pitched cry, limpness, and lethargy
- Difficulty in feeding
- Eye rolling
- Episodes of sweating
- Any unexplained clinical feature in a newborn with a diabetic mother

## Danger signs

Refer the newborn to a higher centre with 10% dextrose IV infusion drip (100 ml/kg/day) if:

- Two values of blood sugar are <20 mg/dL after starting 10% dextrose IV infusion.
- Occurrence of seizures
- The newborn is unable to suck at repeated attempts and blood sugar is <20 mg/dL.
- Failure to maintain IV line and blood sugar is <20 mg/dL.

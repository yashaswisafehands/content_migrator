# Chapter: Contraception following abortion

All women undergoing an abortion should be offered counselling on contraceptive methods. Following contraceptive methods are recommended:

- **Hormonal methods** (including pills, injections, implants, the patch and vaginal ring) may be started immediately after any abortion.
- **Condom.**Use may start with the first intercourse after abortion incl. septic abortion.

![Image](b0bccf4280da47668bdd1dcb20691bf1)

- **IUDs.**Should **NOT** be inserted immediately after septic abortion. Higher risk of expulsion when inserted after second trimester abortion than first trimester abortion.

![Image](80420b28af6d4440ae0eaf4786ce8e1e)

- **Diaphragm or cervical cap.** Use should be postponed for 6 weeks following abortion beyond 14 weeks.

![Image](7b53b659a1a049999adac89245ac430e)

- **Fertility-awareness-based methods.** Should be delayed until regular menstrual cycle returns.

![Image](9ffa2ce5328d4a1b9dc436a35077ce04)

- **Emergency contraception**. Such as pills or IUD can be used within 5 days of  unprotected sexual intercourse to decrease the risk of getting pregnant.

# Chapter: Nutritional supplements for LBW babies

1. Supplements are required for low birth weight newborns and should be started once they are accepting full oral feeds.
2. **Vitamin D:** All LBW infants who are exclusively breastfed should receive 400 I U daily of vitamin D from first few days of life once they accept full feeds. The supplementation should continue till one year of age.
3. **Multivitamin drops with Zinc:** 1ml /day from 2 weeks of age till 40 weeks.
4. **Calcium and phosphorous:** All Very Low birth weight babies (< 1500 gms) should receive elemental calcium and phosphorous at 120-160mg/Kg/day and 60-80mg/Kg/day respectively. This may be continued till 40 weeks post conceptual age. For optimal supplementation, the preparations having the calcium and phosphorous in 2:1 should be used.
5. **Iron:** Elemental iron supplementation at 2 mg/Kg/day at 2 weeks in <1500 grams is effective in preventing anaemia of prematurity till one year of age. Subsequently all infants should receive iron supplementation from the age of 6 months onwards.
